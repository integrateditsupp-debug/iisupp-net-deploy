## 1. The strategy (the Garry Tan / gstack move)

gstack went viral because it **gave away the method** (23 Claude-Code "specialists" as open-source slash commands) and let the credibility sell everything downstream. We do the same:

- **Give away a free "method"** — a small, genuinely useful open-source pack — to earn distribution and trust.
- **Funnel that attention** into the paid Growth Library, ARIA subscriptions, and IIS services.
- Every video is a 3–8 minute *demonstration* of a problem we solve, ending with a free thing (the open-source pack / a free peek) and a paid upgrade (the full guide / ARIA / a service).

**The free lead-magnet to open-source (do this first):**
> **"IIS Helpdesk Starter — free L1 triage prompts + KB skeleton"**
> A public GitHub repo: ~10 ready prompt templates (ticket summary, SLA-risk check, suggested-fix) + a blank KB SOP template + a sample of 3 troubleshooting articles. MIT-ish license. README links to the paid Growth Library packs and ARIA.

This is the top of the funnel. The depth (the full 9 products, ARIA, managed services) is the monetization.

## 6. Product Research & Creation Engine (how we pick the next product)

Run this loop monthly. The output slots straight into `assets/iis-catalog.js` as a new object.

**Find demand (free tools):** YouTube search autocomplete, Google "people also ask", Reddit (r/sysadmin, r/ITCareerQuestions, r/msp), AnswerThePublic-style questions, and our own ARIA ticket patterns (real, proprietary signal — what people actually ask us).

For each candidate, answer:
- Who buys it, and what painful/expensive problem does it remove?
- What exact search term finds it? Is competition weak or generic?
- What makes OUR version sharper (real workflows, AI-readable version, templates)?
- What does it bundle with? Can it become a subscription / team license / MSP license?
- Does it feed ARIA, services, or enterprise? (It should.)

**Scoring (1–5 each, ship if ≥18/25):** Demand · Pain · Differentiation · Bundle/▲-path · Strategic fit to IIS/ARIA.

**Ladder to scale (build every product to climb this):**
> one sale → bundle → membership (All-Access) → team/business license → MSP/school license → enterprise + AI-platform licensing of the AI-readable KBs.

**Next 8 candidates to research:** ServiceNow/ticketing SOP pack · Intune/MDM enrolment KB · Entra ID admin runbook · "AI for accountants/lawyers/clinics" vertical prompt packs · Phishing simulation kit · Incident-response one-pager · SaaS onboarding/offboarding checklist · "ARIA-ready" KB licensing for other MSPs.
