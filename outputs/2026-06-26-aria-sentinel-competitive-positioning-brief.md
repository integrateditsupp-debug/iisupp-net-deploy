## Key Objections and Responses

**"We already have a guy for IT."**
> "That's great — we work well alongside in-house IT. ARIA can handle the routine tickets so your person focuses on bigger projects. And when they're unavailable, we're the backup."

**"It's too expensive."**
> "What does one emergency call cost you in downtime and hourly rates? Most clients find our monthly plan costs less than two unplanned calls. And it covers everything in between."

**"We need someone on-site."**
> "We handle most issues remotely — 80%+ of tickets are resolved that way. When onsite is needed, we coordinate it. You're not paying travel time for a password reset."

**"We want a bigger company with a support team."**
> "Bigger doesn't mean faster. Large MSPs triage by contract tier — SMBs often wait. We answer directly. If you want, let's do a test run — compare response time."

**"We're worried about our data with AI."**
> "ARIA Sentinel runs locally on your machines — your data doesn't leave your environment. We can show you the architecture. No cloud dependency for the AI triage layer."

**"What's your track record?"**
> "We're transparent about being early-stage. We have active deployments, measured self-test data (68% precision on our own KB, improving), and we're building reference cases now. That's why our pricing is competitive — you get founder-level attention and help us build the proof."
