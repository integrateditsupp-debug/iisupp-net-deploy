Questions people ask us

Is this an audit of my tenant?

No. It cannot see your tenant. It is a self-assessment scored from your own answers — it tells you which known oversharing exposures you have not yet closed, and which one to close first. If you want the real thing measured inside your tenant, that is a paid engagement and we will say so plainly.

Does anything I type here leave my device?

No. The page is static HTML and JavaScript. There is no form submission, no fetch call, no analytics on the answers, and no email gate. You can disconnect from the internet and it still works.

Do I have to fix everything before enabling Copilot?

No — you have to fix the reachable-and-sensitive intersection first. Most tenants have far more oversharing than they can clean in a quarter. The pragmatic order is: contain Copilot's search scope, kill the "Anyone" links, fix the site-level Everyone grants, then label the content that actually matters. Boiling the ocean is how Copilot rollouts stall for a year.

Where can I read Microsoft's own guidance?

Start with Microsoft's documentation on Copilot data, privacy and security and on Restricted SharePoint Search, which is the built-in way to constrain Copilot's reach while you clean up. We link to the source rather than paraphrase it.
