Reviewer notes. Text & logs are matched against the offline knowledge base on our own iisupp.net function — no third-party AI model, $0 cost, and nothing stored (the text does POST to our server; it is not "on your device"). Images call Claude Fable 5 vision only after you approve the disclosure, and only when ARIA_VISION_MODEL is configured on the deployment — otherwise ARIA honestly abstains rather than guess (Rule 14). For text/logs, PII and secrets are redacted before matching. For images, the picture is sent unredacted to the vision model — pixels can't be masked like text; only the text ARIA reads back is filtered. The privacy receipt at the bottom of each answer shows exactly what was removed and what left the device. A confident diagnosis attaches a one-click Fix that runs through the existing gated Sentinel resolve flow (restore point · kill-switch · signed audit token · logged).
