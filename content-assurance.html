Commercial model

Unlock the bundle

The on-screen preview is always free. You only pay to unlock the delivery bundle — PDF, ZIP, and JSON.

  • $5per report Single unlock One report, no subscription. For occasional reviews and pilot testing.
  • $20per month Monthly · 20 credits Best value Twenty report credits per 30-day cycle — $1 a report, a fifth of the single-unlock price.
  • $2per extra Overage credit One additional report once an active monthly allowance is used up.

Credits are tied to the checkout email and expire at the end of the 30-day cycle. Unused credits do not roll over in this MVP.

Only minimal billing and usage metadata persist. Source text and extracted PII are not stored as reusable artifacts.
