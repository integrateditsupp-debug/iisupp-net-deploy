Professional Biography

Over 15 years of hands-on experience inside the kinds of organizations most IT firms only hope to support: RBC, Scotiabank, Ontario Health, the City of Toronto, IBM, Cavalluzzo LLP, and other enterprise environments. Ahmad W., Founder & CEO of Integrated IT Support Inc., built IIS from the inside out after operating at Team Lead, Major Incident Manager, and senior technical-services levels across financial, healthcare, public-sector, legal, and global enterprise operations.

That background includes close work around RBC capital-markets and other high-accountability programs, building a practical understanding of institutional culture, workflow discipline, process controls, and policy expectations. IIS does not borrow enterprise language for marketing; it applies enterprise-tested methods because its leadership has already worked under them in production.

The company also takes deliberate time to study the sectors it serves before taking work on, then carries those lessons into scoped, high-quality delivery for smaller teams and growing businesses. Since launching Integrated IT Support Inc. in May 2023, the standard has remained the same: controlled execution, clear accountability, and quality work without overpromising.
