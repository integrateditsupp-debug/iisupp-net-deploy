## The one-sentence position
The enterprise AI-support platforms **deflect chat in Slack/Teams for companies with thousands of seats at six-figure contracts**. ARIA Sentinel **acts on the actual Windows endpoint — gated, reversible, fully audited — for SMBs, starting with a free 14-day pilot and a 5-minute install.**

## Where we win (all verifiable in the product today)
- **Acts on the endpoint, not just chat.** ARIA applies the fix on the device (Manual / Confirmed / Autonomous), with a **restore point before every fix**, a **Ctrl+Alt+K kill-switch**, and a **tamper-evident hash-chained audit log**. The incumbents primarily *route/answer/deflect* inside Slack/Teams or the ITSM portal.
- **Honesty moat (Rule 14).** ARIA shows **real-or-empty** metrics — an empty dashboard reads "—", never a fabricated number. This is a deliberate, defensible trust position an enterprise black-box dashboard can't easily claim.
- **SMB economics + a real free pilot.** 14-day pilot, **no account required to start**, SMB pricing. The three incumbents are enterprise, sales-led, custom-quote, **no advertised free tier** (see sources).
- **Time-to-value in minutes.** 5-minute install, **KB-first at $0** (280+ articles), cloud AI only on fallthrough, **offline brain** when disconnected — the user always gets an answer.
- **Human backup.** A real IT company (Integrated IT Support Inc.) stands behind the software — white-glove for the accounts that want it.

## Where they are strong (be honest with the buyer)
- Deep enterprise integrations, mature multi-department automation (IT+HR+facilities), large reference logos, big-team change management. For a 5,000-seat enterprise standardizing on one ITSM, an incumbent may fit. **Our lane is the SMB they price out** — lead there.

## Objection handling
- *"Why not Moveworks/Aisera/ServiceNow?"* → Those are enterprise platforms priced for thousands of seats (Moveworks median ≈ $130K/yr; ServiceNow 3-yr TCO ≈ $215K at 50 users). For an SMB that's overkill — ARIA gives you an agent that *fixes the machine* for SMB money, and you can start a **free pilot today**.
- *"Is your AI just another chatbot?"* → No. ARIA answers KB-first at $0, and when you approve it, it **runs the fix on the device** with a restore point and a kill-switch. It's an IT department, not a chat window.
- *"How do I trust the numbers?"* → We show real-or-empty. If ARIA hasn't fixed anything yet, the dashboard says so. Your ROI one-pager fills in from *your* pilot's real activity — not our marketing.
