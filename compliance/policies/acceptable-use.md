# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

**Owner:** Ahmad Wasee, CISO  |  **Version:** 1.0  |  **Effective:** 2026-06-15  |  **Review:** Annual

## Purpose

Define acceptable use of ARIA Provider systems, customer-facing services, and ARIA AI assistant. Applies to all ARIA Provider personnel, contractors, and customer end-users.

## Prohibited use

Users may not:

### Security & integrity
- Bypass, disable, or circumvent security controls, rate limits, or query quotas.
- Probe, scan, or test vulnerability of the Service without express written permission.
- Reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Service.
- Introduce malicious code, viruses, trojans, worms, or harmful agents.
- Interfere with or disrupt the Service or networks connected to it.
- Use the Service for credential stuffing, brute-force attacks, or other malicious automation.

### Legal & ethical
- Use the Service to generate, store, or transmit unlawful content.
- Infringe intellectual property rights of others.
- Use the Service for phishing, fraud, identity theft, or scams.
- Generate, distribute, or facilitate child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
- Generate hate speech, incitement to violence, or harassment.
- Use the Service to circumvent export controls or sanctions.
- Use the Service for stalking, doxxing, or non-consensual surveillance.

### Commercial & data
- Resell or sublicense the Service except as expressly authorized in Schedule A of the MSA.
- Use the Service to train competing AI models.
- Scrape, harvest, or extract bulk data without authorization.
- Use Customer Data of one customer to benefit another.

### High-risk applications
- Use ARIA as the sole decision-maker for legally consequential actions (medical diagnosis, legal advice, financial advice, hiring, lending, insurance underwriting) without qualified human review.
- Use ARIA in life-critical systems (aviation control, medical life support, nuclear systems).

## Required behavior

Users must:
- Comply with applicable laws and regulations.
- Protect their access credentials.
- Report suspected violations to integrateditsupp@iisupp.net subject "AUP Violation".
- Cooperate with security investigations.
- Use ARIA responses as informational guidance, not as professional legal/medical/financial advice.

## Customer responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:
- Educating their end-users on this AUP.
- Monitoring for AUP violations within their tenant.
- Promptly addressing violations and notifying ARIA Provider of material breaches.

## Enforcement

### Detection
- Automated rate-limit and abuse detection (Aperture).
- Customer reports.
- Sub-processor notifications.
- Public reports.

### Response
- **Minor:** warning to user + customer admin.
- **Moderate:** temporary suspension; remediation required.
- **Severe:** immediate suspension; investigation; potential MSA termination.
- **Illegal:** report to authorities as required by law.

### Appeal
- Customer admin may appeal enforcement actions to CISO within 14 days.
- Decision communicated within 5 business days.

## Right to refuse / suspend

ARIA Provider reserves the right to refuse service or suspend access for any user or customer violating this AUP, in addition to remedies in the MSA.
