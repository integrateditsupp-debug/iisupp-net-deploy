Your data — access & deletion

Aligned to PIPEDA Principle 9 (Individual Access) and GDPR right-to-erasure. Two tools below.

0 · Prove it is your mailbox

Exports and deletions only run for a verified email: we send a one-time sign-in link (valid 15 minutes, no password). The link opens /aria and signs you in for 12 hours on this browser; then come back here.

Checking sign-in…

1 · Export everything ARIA has on you

We dump every server-side record (Stripe account, invoices) AND every browser-side localStorage key we set. Download as JSON.


    

  


  

    
2 · Delete everything

    
Cancels active subscriptions, flags Stripe customer as deleted, sends confirmation email. You must also click Log Out on /aria to clear browser-side data.
