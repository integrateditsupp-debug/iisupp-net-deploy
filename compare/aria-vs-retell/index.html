ARIA vs Retell AI

A phone agent solves calls. ARIA starts with the support problem.

Retell AI is positioned for building, testing, deploying, and monitoring AI phone agents with telephony, prompts, tools, and analytics. ARIA is aimed at the repeat IT-support layer: troubleshooting, knowledge lookup, user-safe guidance, and escalation handoff.

Pick ARIA when
  • You want fewer internal tickets and clearer first answers.
  • The problem is not primarily phone calls.
  • You need a support product that speaks IT before it speaks sales.
Pick Retell when
  • Your business case is call automation at scale.
  • You want a platform focused on deploying and monitoring AI phone agents.
  • You already know the exact call flows to automate.
IIS angle

ARIA can qualify the operational pain first. If calls become the strongest ROI path, IIS can scope the voice-agent lane as a separate build instead of mixing two different jobs.

Verdict

For internal IT support and SMB help desk conversion, ARIA is the sharper landing page offer. Retell is stronger when the paid requirement is clearly phone-agent operations.
