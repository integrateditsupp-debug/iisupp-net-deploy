ARIA vs Retell AI

Choose IT-support workflow before voice infrastructure.

If the buyer wants to build a custom voice agent, a voice infrastructure platform may be the right technical route. If the buyer needs IT triage, knowledge answers, escalation, and service handoff now, ARIA is the cleaner starting point.

Use ARIA when

The evaluation is really about support outcomes.

  • Users need IT answers. The problem is password resets, M365, VPN, devices, printers, email, policy, and escalation clarity.
  • The buyer wants a support wrapper. IIS can provide setup, tuning, governance, and human backup rather than leaving a raw platform to configure.
  • The workflow needs guardrails. ARIA can present safe handoff language, triage structure, and support-specific prompts.
  • The first win must be small. A scoped ARIA pilot can validate ticket deflection before a custom voice build is funded.
