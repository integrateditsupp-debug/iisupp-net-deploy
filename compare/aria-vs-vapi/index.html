ARIA vs Vapi

Voice infrastructure is not the same as an IT support brain.

Vapi is a developer platform for building voice AI agents that can make and receive phone calls, connect with APIs, and handle workflows. ARIA is a support-product lane for IT questions, company knowledge, escalation discipline, and employee self-service.

Pick ARIA when
  • The first pain is repeat IT questions, not phone volume.
  • You need a guided support intake before a human ticket.
  • Company KB/privacy posture matters more than voice-agent customization.
Pick Vapi when
  • You have developers ready to build and maintain a custom voice agent.
  • Your main workflow starts or ends on phone calls.
  • You need deep telephony and API orchestration control.
IIS angle

ARIA can be the front door. IIS can still scope a Vapi-style build later if phone automation becomes the paid need, without pretending every business needs that first.

Verdict

For Google Ads aimed at IT support, Microsoft 365 friction, employee productivity, and small-business help desk pain, ARIA is the clearer starting offer. Vapi becomes attractive when the prospect explicitly wants to build a phone agent.
