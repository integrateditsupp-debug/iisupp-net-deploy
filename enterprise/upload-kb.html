Upload your company KB to ARIA

Paste your internal docs, runbooks, policies, FAQs — anything ARIA should reference when answering your team's questions. Ahmad reviews, formats, and merges into your tenant's KB within 1-2 business days. Available on Small Business, Mid-Size, and Enterprise plans.

What happens next

Within 1-2 business days Ahmad reviews the content for formatting, normalizes it into ARIA's KB schema, and merges it into your tenant's KB. After that, every time your team asks ARIA a question with their work email, ARIA references YOUR docs first before falling back to the general KB. You get a confirmation email when ingestion is complete.
