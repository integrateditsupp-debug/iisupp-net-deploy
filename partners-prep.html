Foundation (do these first — free or near-free)

D-U-N-S Number FREE

Required for both Microsoft Cloud Partner Program and AWS Partner Network. Issued by Dun & Bradstreet. Processing: ~30 days.

Why: most partner programs auto-verify your business with this. Without it = manual review queue = weeks added.

Verify CRA business number on record FREE

Make sure your IIS CRA Business Number (BN) is current. Partner verifications cross-check against CRA records.

Microsoft Partner Center account FREE

Create the master Microsoft account that owns your partner relationship. Use a business email, NOT personal Gmail.

AWS account + Partner Central FREE

Root AWS account with payment method on file (required even for free tier). Register at AWS Partner Central.
