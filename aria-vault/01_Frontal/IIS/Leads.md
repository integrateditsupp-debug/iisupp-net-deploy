## Rules for adding a lead here

1. **Never** type the lead's name. Use the next free `Lead-NNN`.
2. Keep the row to: handle · status · stage · next step · source. No bio, no contact info.
3. Real contact info stays in: Apollo CRM · Gmail Drafts · WhatsApp (out of vault).
4. If you need to brief Cowork/Codex on a specific lead, do it in chat — not as a vault note.

## Why

- Vault is the graph view + shared agent memory. Real customer names in graph = privacy exposure if anyone screenshots the graph (already happens for demos / docs / fundraising).
- CRM is the system of record. Vault is the operating brain.
- Anonymization keeps the brain useful without the leak surface.
