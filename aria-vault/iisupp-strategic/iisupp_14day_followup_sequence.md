# 14-Day Multi-Touch Follow-Up Sequence

For prospects who got the cold outreach email (Day 0) and didn't respond. Most B2B deals close on touch 5-12, not touch 1. This sequence is the difference between a 1% response rate and a 15-20% engagement rate.

**Audience:** The Pacific 14 cohort (and every future cohort). Adapt voice per region using the variants from the regional-email exemplar.

**Channel rotation:** Email → LinkedIn DM → Email → Voicemail → Email → LinkedIn forward → "Breakup" email.

---

## Day 0 — Original cold email (already sent ✅)

The personalized email with first-name salutation, specific observation, diagnostic offer, risk reversal. Don't restart the clock from here — they got this, they ignored it. Now we start the follow-up.

---

## Day 3 — LinkedIn connection request (no message)

**Action:** Send LinkedIn connection request. NO custom message — just the default request.

**Why:** This is reconnaissance. If they accept the connection within 48 hours without context, they're either curious about you or low-friction connectors. Either is good. If they don't accept, you have your answer — drop them.

**Time to execute:** 90 seconds per prospect.

---

## Day 5 — Short follow-up email

**Subject:** `re: [original subject]` (keeps the thread)

**Body:**
```
[FirstName],

Bumping this up in case it got buried — totally understand if 
the timing is wrong.

The 1-page diagnostic offer still stands. Even if you'd just rather 
chat about how other firms are thinking about AI workflows right now, 
I'm happy to share what we're seeing.

Ahmad
iisupp.net
```

**Why:** 38 words. Acknowledges they're busy. Restates the offer once. Opens a softer side door ("just rather chat"). Doesn't apologize.

---

## Day 8 — LinkedIn DM (only if they accepted Day 3 connection)

**Message:**
```
[FirstName] — sent you a note last week about [specific 
observation]. No reply needed if it's not a fit. But if you ever 
want to compare notes on how AI is showing up in [their industry — 
law / accounting / property mgmt / etc] in 2026, the door is open.

iisupp.net if you're curious. — Ahmad
```

**Why:** Different channel = different vibe. References the email so they connect the dots. Permission-based ("no reply needed"). Plants curiosity without pressure.

---

## Day 9 — Voicemail (if you have or can find a number)

**Script (60 seconds, sounds natural — practice once, don't read):**

> "Hi [FirstName], this is Ahmad Wasee, founder of Integrated IT Support up in Whitby, Ontario. Sent you a note last week — no urgency at all to call back. I'm just reaching out because I saw [specific observation] at [Company] and thought there might be a 15-minute conversation worth having about how AI is changing [their workflow]. If it's of interest, I'm at six-four-seven, five-eight-one, three-one-eight-two. Otherwise, ignore this entirely. Have a good week."

**Why:** Voicemails — yes, voicemails in 2026 — are wildly under-used. They reach the prospect personally, in their car, in their kitchen, when email is closed. The "no urgency" framing removes pressure. The repetition of the phone number means they can save it without rewinding.

---

## Day 11 — Value-add forward (no ask)

**Subject:** Brief follow-up — saw something I thought you'd want to see

**Body:**
```
[FirstName],

No pitch — just saw this and thought of you given [Company]'s 
[specific work area].

[One-paragraph summary of a relevant article, data point, 
regulatory change, or trend specific to their industry. NOT a 
sales angle. Pure useful intel.]

Source: [link]

That's it. No reply needed. — Ahmad
```

**Why:** This is the unlock. After 4 touches where you asked for something, you give something. Free. No ask. Most prospects will respond out of reflex — "thanks for sending, this is actually useful." That reply opens the door to actual conversation.

**The trick:** Have a "weekly intelligence" folder you maintain for each industry vertical — clippings, articles, data points worth forwarding. Building this once gives you ammunition for years of these touches.

---

## Day 14 — "Breakup" email

**Subject:** Last note — should I close the loop?

**Body:**
```
[FirstName],

I've reached out a few times — your inbox is full enough as it 
is, so this is my last note unless you want to keep talking.

If "not now" is the answer, no worries. If "not ever," even better 
— just reply with an N and I'll mark you down and stop appearing 
in your inbox.

If you're curious about that diagnostic offer or just want to 
chat about AI in [their industry] sometime, the door's always 
open: iisupp.net.

Best,
Ahmad
```

**Why:** Counter-intuitively, "breakup" emails have the **highest reply rate** in the sequence — typically 15-30%. The "closing the loop" frame triggers loss aversion and respect ("ok he's not going to badger me, let me give him a real answer"). Half the replies will be "yes let's chat" or "send me the diagnostic info."

The N reply rule is crucial — give them an OUT. Most people respect the request and reply N. That's still useful: you keep your list clean, and a few of them will reply "actually let me think about it" instead.

---

## What happens after Day 14

- If they replied at any point → move to the **discovery call sequence** (separate doc, but key: don't pitch on call 1, diagnose only)
- If they replied "N" → archive, never contact again. Honor it.
- If they didn't reply at all → quiet archive. Wait 6 months. Then run a completely different angle (case study, congratulations on a hire/funding, etc).

---

## How to execute this at scale

**For a cohort of 15:**
- Day 0: already done
- Day 3: 15 × 90 sec = 23 min (LinkedIn connects)
- Day 5: 15 × 60 sec = 15 min (short follow-up emails — drafted via Gmail MCP)
- Day 8: ~8 × 2 min = 16 min (LinkedIn DMs, only the ~50% who accepted connection)
- Day 9: 8-15 × 3 min = 24-45 min (voicemails — can be partially automated via Skype/Twilio or recorded one-shot)
- Day 11: 15 × 2 min = 30 min (value-add forwards — one industry-specific article per vertical)
- Day 14: 15 × 60 sec = 15 min (breakup emails)

**Total time across 14 days: ~2.5 hours of work for a 15-person cohort.**

Expected engagement: 3-5 of 15 will reply or accept some touch. 1-2 of those will become real conversations. **Compared to a 0% reply rate on a 1640-email blast, the math is obvious.**

---

## Tracking

For each prospect, log in a CSV:
- Original send date + subject
- Day 3 connection status
- Day 5 follow-up sent
- Day 8 DM sent (if applicable)
- Day 9 voicemail status
- Day 11 forward sent
- Day 14 breakup sent
- Reply status + date + content summary
- Next action

This becomes your living pipeline.
