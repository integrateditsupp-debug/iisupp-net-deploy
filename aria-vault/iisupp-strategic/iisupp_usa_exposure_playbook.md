# PART 1 — FEDERAL PROCUREMENT PORTALS

> **Canadian-business reality check up front:** SAM.gov registration for direct federal prime contracting requires a US business presence (US address + EIN). However: (a) Canadian companies CAN register as foreign vendors under the Defense Production Act sharing framework and Canadian Goods Authorization (CGAuth); (b) MANY federal opportunities are open to subcontracting from Canada; (c) GSA Schedule is harder cross-border but possible via partnership with a US prime; (d) some platforms (USAspending.gov, FPDS) are RESEARCH portals — no registration needed, but enormously useful for spotting active buyers.
>
> **The most efficient path for IIS Inc. right now is research + subcontracting, not direct prime contracting.** If Ahmad wants to pursue US federal direct work at scale, he should register a Delaware C-corp subsidiary (~$500-1,200 to incorporate, ~$300/year ongoing) and migrate procurement registrations into it.

## 1.1 SAM.gov (System for Award Management)
- **URL:** https://sam.gov
- **What it is:** The mandatory federal registration database for any entity wanting to do business with the US Government as a prime contractor.
- **Buyer audience:** ~750,000+ active federal buyers across 100+ agencies. ~$700B in annual federal contract spend flows through SAM-registered entities.
- **Registration prerequisites:**
  - Unique Entity ID (UEI) — replaced DUNS in April 2022. Free, applied for AT SAM.gov.
  - US Tax ID (EIN) — Canadian businesses can get an EIN by filing Form SS-4 with the IRS; takes 4-8 weeks by mail or fax. NO US presence is required to apply for an EIN.
  - CAGE Code (Commercial and Government Entity Code) — auto-assigned during SAM registration for US entities. Foreign entities are assigned an NCAGE code via the NATO Support and Procurement Agency (apply at https://eportal.nspa.nato.int/).
  - Banking: US bank account preferred for direct deposit, but not strictly required for foreign registrants.
- **Time to register:** HIGH (4-8 weeks total: NCAGE first 1-3 weeks, then SAM 1-2 weeks, plus EIN if needed)
- **Cost:** FREE (anyone charging you is a scam — SAM is government-run)
- **IIS-specific notes:**
  - Ahmad can register IIS Inc as a FOREIGN vendor. This works for subcontracting and Defense Production Sharing.
  - For prime contracting on most opportunities, US business presence is functionally required.
  - **Recommended NAICS codes for IIS:** 541511 (Custom Computer Programming), 541512 (Computer Systems Design), 541519 (Other Computer Services), 541611 (Administrative Management Consulting), 541618 (Other Management Consulting), 518210 (Data Processing/Hosting), 541715 (R&D in Physical/Engineering Sciences for AI work).
- **Step-by-step:**
  1. Apply for NCAGE at https://eportal.nspa.nato.int/AC135Public/CageTool/home (foreign business code)
  2. Apply for US EIN via IRS Form SS-4 (fax to +1-855-215-1627 for foreign applicants)
  3. Register at https://sam.gov/content/entity-registration (UEI auto-assigned)
  4. Complete entity registration with NAICS codes above
  5. Set up "Notifications" to alert on new opportunities matching NAICS
- **Priority for IIS:** MEDIUM (foundational but slow payoff; do this in parallel with faster channels)

## 1.2 GSA eBuy + GSA Schedule (Multiple Award Schedule)
- **URL:** https://www.ebuy.gsa.gov | Schedule application: https://www.gsa.gov/buy-through-us/purchasing-programs/multiple-award-schedule
- **What it is:** The General Services Administration's pre-negotiated, government-wide contract vehicle. Once you're "on Schedule," federal buyers can purchase from you via eBuy without re-competing.
- **Buyer audience:** ~150,000 federal buyers across all civilian + defense agencies. ~$45B+/yr flows through GSA Schedule contracts.
- **Registration prerequisites:**
  - Active SAM.gov registration (prerequisite — see 1.1)
  - 2 years in business OR a strong "Startup Springboard" application (GSA's exception for newer firms with demonstrated capability)
  - $25K+ in past commercial sales OR 2+ verifiable client past-performance references
  - Detailed pricing proposal (Commercial Sales Practices disclosure)
- **Time to register:** HIGH (6-12 months from application to award — this is the long pole)
- **Cost:** Free to apply. Industrial Funding Fee (IFF) of 0.75% on all sales once on Schedule.
- **IIS-specific notes:**
  - The "2 years in business" requirement is a problem. IIS Inc. was founded ~2023 — may qualify with documentation by late 2026.
  - **Startup Springboard exception** is the lane for IIS: requires demonstrated technical capability (ARIA platform is gold here), founder past performance (Ahmad's 20+ years employment record), and a single anchor commercial reference (Tier-1 diagnostic client when one closes).
  - Cross-border: GSA Schedule is HARD for Canadian-only entities. Best path is to partner with an existing US prime as a subcontractor, build past-performance, then apply 2027+.
- **Step-by-step:**
  1. Confirm SAM.gov active
  2. Review "Pathway to Success" training (mandatory, free): https://www.gsa.gov/cdnstatic/Pathway_to_Success.pdf
  3. Draft Commercial Sales Practices disclosure
  4. Submit via GSA eOffer: https://eoffer.gsa.gov
  5. Plan for 6-12 month negotiation cycle
- **Priority for IIS:** LOW for 2026 (defer to 2027 once SAM + past-performance are mature)
