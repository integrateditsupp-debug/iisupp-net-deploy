# Integrated IT Support — 3 Productized Offers

Replace the current "PICK A PLAN" / "PURCHASE · DEPOSIT" / "INSTALL APP" buttons on iisupp.net with three concrete, scoped, priced packages. The goal is to remove all "let me get a quote" friction — buyer sees the menu, picks their fit, signs.

---

## TIER 1 — AI READINESS DIAGNOSTIC
### $1,950 CAD · 5-business-day delivery · Fully refundable

**Who it's for:** Founders, COOs, or office leads who suspect AI/automation could help but don't know where to start.

**What you get:**
- 45-min discovery call (your team + ours)
- Full audit of your top 3 manual workflows (intake, scheduling, document review, vendor coordination, etc.)
- 12-page written diagnostic with:
  - Specific time + cost wasted per workflow (with numbers)
  - 3 prioritized AI/automation moves with expected ROI
  - 30/60/90-day implementation roadmap
- 60-min walkthrough of the diagnostic with your team
- All artifacts yours to keep, no NDA strings

**Risk reversal:** If the diagnostic doesn't surface at least 20 hours/month of recoverable time across your top 3 workflows, you get a full refund. No questions, no claw-back.

**Why this exists:** Most consultancies charge $15-50K for a "discovery phase" with vague deliverables. We do the same depth in 5 days for under $2K because we're using our own ARIA agents to accelerate the analysis. You get the same intel without the McKinsey markup.

→ **[BOOK DIAGNOSTIC]** button

## TIER 2 — AI PILOT (90-DAY BUILD)
### $18,500 CAD · 90-day delivery · 50% guarantee

**Who it's for:** Firms ready to deploy one production AI workflow — not a slide deck, not a "POC", actual running automation.

**What you get:**
- Everything in Tier 1
- One production AI workflow built, deployed, and running in your environment
- Choose from: AI document pre-screening, AI intake automation, AI scheduling consolidation, AI vendor/RFP triage, AI compliance review pre-check
- Built on Anthropic Claude + MCP architecture (the same stack we use for ARIA)
- 30-day post-launch monitoring + tuning
- Knowledge transfer to one of your team members
- Full source code + documentation (you own it)

**Risk reversal:** If the deployed workflow doesn't measurably save your team at least 30 hours/month at the 90-day checkpoint, we refund 50% of the fee AND continue tuning at no charge until the metric is hit.

**Why this exists:** Most "AI implementation" projects are PowerPoints, not running code. We ship working production AI in 90 days because we've built our own internal platform (ARIA, 9+ scheduled agents in production daily) — we're not learning on your dime.

→ **[START YOUR PILOT]** button

## TIER 3 — AI INTEGRATION PARTNER (RETAINED)
### $4,800 CAD/month · 6-month minimum · Quarterly business review

**Who it's for:** Firms that have proven the ROI on a pilot and want IIS as their ongoing AI/IT partner across multiple workflows.

**What you get:**
- Continuous build + maintenance of 3-5 production AI workflows
- Monthly health check + performance optimization
- New workflow scoping + delivery (1 new workflow per quarter included)
- Priority response (4-hour SLA business hours)
- Quarterly Business Review with measurable outcomes
- Access to Ahmad direct for strategic AI/IT calls (founder line, not a junior account manager)

**Risk reversal:** Quarter-by-quarter cancellation after the initial 6 months. No long-term lock-in beyond the initial commitment. If we fail to ship the quarterly workflow, that month is free.

**Why this exists:** Most MSPs and consultancies upsell you for years on advisory hours with no shipped output. We commit to one shipped production workflow per quarter, or you don't pay for that month. The relationship is held together by output, not invoices.

## Pricing rationale

- **$1,950 Diagnostic** — high enough to filter tire-kickers, low enough to be a no-brainer for a serious buyer evaluating $20-100K of automation
- **$18,500 Pilot** — at $200/hour fully-loaded rate, this is ~92 hours of work — realistic for a focused 90-day single-workflow build
- **$4,800/month Retainer** — equivalent to ~24 hours/month of senior consulting (going market rate $200/hr) PLUS the quarterly workflow delivery — competitive vs hiring a fractional CIO ($8-15K/month) or boutique MSP retainer ($6-12K/month)

All prices in CAD. For UK/UAE/USA prospects, multiply by relevant FX rate (~0.74 GBP / 2.7 AED / 0.73 USD) and round to local convention ($1,500 USD / £1,450 / 5,500 AED for Tier 1, etc).

## What this unlocks

- **Email outreach** can now reference specific pricing: "We have a $1,950 diagnostic that maps your top 3 automation candidates in 5 days — fully refundable if we don't find 20+ hrs/month of recoverable time."
- **Discovery calls** become shorter — buyer has already seen the tiers, knows the menu
- **Closing rate** improves because pricing transparency removes the #1 enterprise buyer objection: "will this cost more than I think"
- **Tier 1 → Tier 2 conversion** is the actual revenue play — diagnostics that surface real pain naturally upsell to pilots in ~30-50% of cases
