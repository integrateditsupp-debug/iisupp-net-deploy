# Reply Handling Playbook — USA Outreach Campaign

**Owner:** Ahmad Wasee | Integrated IT Support Inc.
**Purpose:** Same-day, on-brand responses to the 12 most likely reply patterns. Speed of response is the #1 conversion lever — replies within 1 business hour close at ~3x the rate of next-day responses.

**Tone discipline (project rules):**
- Calm, senior-director energy. Never desperate. Never over-eager.
- Match their energy: short reply gets short response; detailed gets thoughtful.
- No buzzwords. No "let's hop on a quick call." No "circling back."
- Always offer the next concrete action (calendar link, document, deeper info).

## DAILY REPLY ROUTINE

1. Check inbox at 8 AM, 12 PM, 4 PM CT (3x/day during outreach campaign)
2. Same-day response to ALL replies — speed compounds
3. Log every reply in `outreach-replies-tracker.csv` with: contact, company, reply category (1–12 above), response sent, next action, next-action date
4. Escalate "yes, send info" and "set up a call" to top of inbox — those are the closes
5. End of week: review reply rates by segment, refine subject lines and openers for next batch
