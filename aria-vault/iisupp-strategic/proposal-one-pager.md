Proactive monitoring, patching, M365 admin, business-hours helpdesk, monthly health audit.

### Tier 2 — Business Continuity *(most-selected)*
**$14,000 – $24,000 / month** | Mid-Size · 11–50 Users
Everything in Tier 1 + 1-hour SLA, quarterly executive IT briefing, dedicated senior analyst, Active Directory/identity management, disaster recovery planning, full vendor coordination.
*Most relevant to:* multi-location dental groups (3–8 practices), urgent care chains (3–6 facilities), mid-market law firms, real estate brokerages.

### Tier 3 — Enterprise Operations
**$30,000 – $60,000 / month** | Corporate · 51–200+ Users · Multi-Location

*Most relevant to:* DSOs (10+ practices), enterprise urgent care chains, multi-state law firms, financial institutions.
