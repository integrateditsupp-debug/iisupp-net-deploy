## Deliverability Protection Notes

- **Cap at 20 sends/day max** from Gmail consumer. We're at 17 to stay safely under.
- **Space 30-60 min between sends** — Gmail flags bursts.
- **Reply to anyone who responds within 2 hours** — engagement signals boost domain reputation.
- **Don't send the same email body twice** — Gmail's classifier checks for similarity. Each draft is unique.
- **Watch for bounces** — if 2+ bounce in a row, pause and verify next addresses before continuing.

## Follow-up timing

- **Day +4 from each send:** soft follow-up to non-repliers (e.g., May 14 sends → May 18 follow-ups)
