## Personalization checklist — every email before send

- [ ] First name in subject and opener
- [ ] Specific, verifiable fact in opening paragraph (location count, software, expansion, hiring, leadership change)
- [ ] HIPAA/BAA referenced for any healthcare target
- [ ] PMS/EMR named correctly (Dentrix vs. Eaglesoft vs. Open Dental for dental; eClinicalWorks vs. Athena vs. Epic for urgent care)
- [ ] No marketing buzzwords ("revolutionary," "cutting-edge," "transform")
- [ ] No "autonomous AI tech support" claims — AI is supporting layer, not headline
- [ ] CTA is light: "15-minute call" or "want the mockup?" — never "let's hop on a quick discovery call"
- [ ] Physical address or opt-out line at bottom for CAN-SPAM
- [ ] Signature uses 647-581-3182 and iisupp.net — never the spam-flag "click here"

## DO NOT phrases (auto-flag and rewrite)

| Phrase | Why it's banned |
|---|---|
| "I'd love to show you what we got" | Reads junior, unprofessional |
| "Many other services on our website" | Generic, lazy CTA, dilutes the pitch |
| "Autonomous AI tech support" | Triggers BS detectors immediately |
| "Revolutionary IT solutions" | Marketing-agency tone |
| "Transform your business" | Cliché, ignored by buyers |
| "We made you a website" (if not literally true) | Credibility bomb on reply |
| "Hope this email finds you well" | Spam-flag opener, instant delete |
| "Just checking in" (standalone) | No value, deletes itself |
