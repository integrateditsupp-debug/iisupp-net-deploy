# LinkedIn DM Playbook — Parallel Channel for USA Outreach

**Purpose:** Run LinkedIn outreach in parallel to email — same 22 prospects, different channel, zero infrastructure dependency. You can execute this TODAY without enrichment, without Workspace, without any approval gates.

**Why this works:**
- Senior IT directors and COOs in healthcare check LinkedIn 3–5x more than they reply to cold email
- LinkedIn doesn't have CAN-SPAM constraints
- No deliverability/domain reputation risk
- Profile credibility (your iisupp.net + Senior Director title + Ontario Health/Raymond James references) is visible automatically
- Direct, premium channel — feels less like marketing

## DM FOLLOW-UP (after connection accepts) — same day or next day

LinkedIn DMs have no character limit. Use longer, value-led messages. Match the email drafts in tone — same observable-pain opener, same credibility anchors, same light CTA.

### DM template (universal structure)

```
{FirstName} — appreciate the connect.

Quick context: I run Integrated IT Support (iisupp.net). 21+ years backing healthcare and corporate IT including Raymond James trading floor support and PHIPA-compliant provincial health systems at Ontario Health scale.

Reason for reaching out: {Specific observation about their company from research — same hook as the email draft for that prospect}

Most {DSOs / urgent care chains / law firms} at {their stage} hit the same wall on the IT side — {one specific operational pain referenced in their email draft}.

We absorb that operational layer without you needing to expand internal headcount. Senior-first model (not L1 chatbot scripts), BAA coverage for healthcare, real engineering bench depth for high-stakes moments.

Worth a 15-min call to compare notes? No pitch — just a fast read on what's typical for your scale.

—Ahmad
```

## DAILY EXECUTION RHYTHM

**Mon–Fri at 9 AM CT (15 min):**
- Send 5 new connection requests (use the 300-char templates above)
- Review LinkedIn for new connection acceptances
- Send DMs to anyone who accepted yesterday
- Reply to any DMs received within 4 hours

**Volume math:**
- 5 connection requests × 5 days = 25/week
- 30–50% acceptance → 8–12 DMs sent per week
- 15–25% DM reply rate → 1–3 conversations per week
- 1–3 conversations × 4 weeks = 4–12 monthly discovery opportunities

**Combined with email campaign (when unblocked):**
- LinkedIn: 4–12 conversations/month
- Email: 8–15 conversations/month
- **Total: 12–27 conversations/month → 2–5 retainers in the 90-day window**

---

## WHAT NOT TO DO

- **Don't pitch in the connection request.** That's why everyone ignores them. The connection request is just to open the door. The DM is where the conversation happens.
- **Don't use LinkedIn's "Get More Connections" automated tools.** Trips bot detection. Manual only.
- **Don't message everyone the same day they accept** — wait 4–24 hours so it doesn't feel automated.
- **Don't include calendar links in the first DM** — feels transactional. Offer the call, let them ask for scheduling.
- **Don't accept spam-y reverse-pitches** — when other IT vendors try to sell to YOU on LinkedIn (and they will once you're more visible), politely decline. Don't waste the inbox attention.
