# USA Outreach — DFW Healthcare & Multi-Location Campaign

**Owner:** Ahmad Wasee — Integrated IT Support Inc.
**Launch date:** May 13, 2026
**Target metro:** Dallas–Fort Worth (DFW)
**Sender (target):** ahmad@iisupp.net (Google Workspace) — see workspace-setup-guide.md
**Fallback sender:** integrateditsupp@gmail.com — capped at 15 hyper-personalized sends/day

## Campaign goal

Land 1–3 new managed-services retainers worth $36K–$240K combined ARR within 90 days by targeting multi-location healthcare and professional-services organizations in DFW that have real, observable IT pain.

## Target mix (80/20 high-value / single-office)

| Segment | % of volume | Buyer titles | Avg contract size |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dental Service Organizations (DSOs), 3+ locations | 40% | COO, Dir. of IT, Dir. of Ops, Practice Ops Manager | $5K–$20K/mo |
| Urgent care / walk-in chains, 3+ locations | 25% | Dir. of Ops, IT Director, CTO, Practice Admin | $3K–$15K/mo |
| Multi-location professional services (law, real estate, accounting), 25–250 emp | 15% | Managing Partner, COO, IT Director, Office Manager | $2K–$10K/mo |
| Single-office practices (validation only) | 20% | Practice Manager, Owner-Dentist, Office Admin | $500–$1,500/mo |

## Positioning anchors

Per the project instructions, IIS is positioned as a **trusted operational partner**, not an MSP commodity:

1. **HIPAA + BAA-covered support** — non-negotiable selling point for healthcare buyers
2. **21+ years engineering experience** — credibility against junior outsourced helpdesks
3. **Practice-management software fluency** — Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, eClinicalWorks, Athena, Epic
4. **Overflow + scaling support** — relief for stressed internal IT, not replacement
5. **AI as enhancement layer** — never the pitch, always positioned as supporting human engineering judgment

## Outreach mechanics

- **Volume cap (Gmail):** 15/day max while sending from integrateditsupp@gmail.com
- **Volume cap (Workspace, post-warmup):** 30–50/day after 10-day warmup on ahmad@iisupp.net
- **Personalization floor:** every email references one verifiable, specific fact about the company (location count, software stack, recent expansion, leadership change, hiring signal)
- **Subject line rule:** conversational, first-name-led, no marketing headlines
- **CAN-SPAM:** every send includes physical address + clear opt-out language
- **Cadence:** 2-step sequence — initial email → follow-up at 4 business days if no reply
- **Stop rule:** 2 sends total, no replies → mark "nurture" and move on

## Daily execution loop (after launch)

1. **Morning (8:00 AM CT):** Cowork scheduled task pulls 30 new enriched prospects (60% DSO / 25% urgent care / 15% multi-loc small biz) and drafts personalized emails.
2. **9:00–9:30 AM:** Ahmad reviews/approves the batch — 5-min skim per email.
3. **9:30 AM–12:00 PM:** Approved batch sent in waves of 5–8 per hour from ahmad@iisupp.net.
4. **Throughout day:** Replies routed to Ahmad's inbox for personal handling within 2 business hours.
5. **End of day:** Cowork updates prospect tracker with replies, books, no-replies.
6. **Day +4:** Follow-up auto-drafted for non-repliers.
