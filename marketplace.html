Bridge checkout flow

Check first. Pay after approval.

This is the correct order path. The buyer gives destination details first. IIS checks vendor shipping and final landed cost. Stripe payment opens only after the buyer sees the final quote, vendor/source details, warranty/return notes, and agrees.

1Buyer selects item lane and enters email, country, city, and postal code.
2IIS checks vendor availability, shipping region, warranty, returns, taxes, duties, and final total.
3Buyer receives final quote with vendor/source disclosure and Stripe checkout link.
4After payment, IIS places the approved order and forwards material vendor updates.

Marketplace terms before payment

  • IIS acts as a sourcing and coordination bridge, not the manufacturer.
  • The buyer receives final price, vendor/source details, shipping limits, warranty notes, return notes, and the IIS sourcing/admin fee before Stripe checkout.
  • Payment is requested only after the buyer approves the final quote.
  • Material vendor updates are forwarded to the buyer. IIS does not rewrite required legal, warranty, safety, payment, or delivery notices.
  • Automatic ordering is allowed only when it follows vendor terms and the buyer-approved quote.
