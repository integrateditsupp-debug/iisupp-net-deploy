# OneDrive or Drive folder exposes student records

## Symptom

The requester reports a education support issue that matches: ferpa, onedrive, google drive, share.

## What ARIA should do first

Revoke sharing, export access list, preserve audit logs, notify privacy owner, and reset folder permissions.

## Guardrails

- Collect only the minimum operational details needed to troubleshoot.
- Do not expose student, patient, employee, or production records in screenshots or notes.
- Do not make privileged changes, production changes, legal claims, or vendor submissions without owner approval.

## Escalation

Escalate when: Student records were shared outside the authorized group.
