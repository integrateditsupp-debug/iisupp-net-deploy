# Integrated IT Support Growth Engine

Private operating plan for Codex, Claude Code, Senior Director Agent, and local agents.

## Mission

Grow Integrated IT Support Inc. into a global IT support, AI implementation, website, tender, and offshore support company.

Ahmad should operate like CEO: approvals, meetings, final judgment, and relationship decisions. Agents should handle research, tracking, drafting, QA, monitoring, and safe reversible work.

## Revenue Streams

1. Remote L1-L3 IT support contracts.
2. Website/no-website and weak-online-presence leads.
3. AI implementation and workflow automation.
4. Corporate expansion, office move-in, overflow IT support, workstation deployment, AV/meeting room setup, and onboarding projects.
5. Government/private tenders through CanadaBuys, MERX, Ontario Tenders, municipal portals, and registered procurement sources.
6. Offshore L1-L3 support and AI assistance delivery model.
7. Revenue/product ideas requiring Ahmad decision: approve, reject, research more, or save for later.

## Operating Rules

- Agents may research, draft, monitor, score, queue, and prepare.
- Agents may build no-cost internal portals, trackers, and reversible website/ARIA improvements.
- Agents may use `ahmad.wasee@iisupp.net` for internal identity and draft work.
- Agents may not send external outreach, submit bids, sign, quote, promise, spend, change credentials, or accept penalties without Ahmad approval.
- Any irreversible paperwork, final tender response, public claim, legal/security commitment, bond, liquidated damages clause, or penalty term is CEO approval required.
