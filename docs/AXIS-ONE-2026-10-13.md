## Design — the merged console

```
.axis-dock (right rail, 480px)
 ├ head      globe-orb mark · AXIS <state> · hear line · wave · ×        (ids unchanged)
 ├ brain     #axisBrain (reasoning availability + voice availability)
 ├ tabs      Talk · Work · To-do · Automate · Approvals · My PC · Inbox · Brief · Claude · Setup   (#axisTabs)
 ├ Talk      #axisPri + #axisLog                                            (original dock)
 ├ other     #axisPanel ← axis-exec.js renders its tabs here; axis-collab.js renders "Claude"
 ├ input     #axisInput · 🎙 push-to-talk (turn flow) · 🔊 · ⌁ wake · Send (always visible)
 └ foot      voice picker · Test · Live call (Aria, two-way)
```
- The orbit globe (`#axisOrbGlobe`) is THE globe. Click → console (Talk). Double-click → live call. The orbit bar gets
  a static live-call button. The dock-head sphere becomes the small orb mark (one sphere on screen).
- `axis-unify.js` is no longer loaded (file kept, documented). Its three useful behaviours live in axis-app.js.
- Events (window): `axis:open {tab,say}` · `axis:say {text}` · `axis:tab {tab}` · `axis:transcript {role,text}` ·
  `axis:live-state {state}`. exec/collab stay standalone-capable (they still build their own UI when `#axisPanel`
  is absent, e.g. a future page).
- Voice: `axisSpeak` → Aria TTS → browser fallback; when the bridge is down the brain badge says so.
