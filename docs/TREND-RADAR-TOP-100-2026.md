## Source Anchors

- Gartner 2026 strategic technology trends: AI-native development platforms, AI supercomputing, confidential computing, multiagent systems, domain-specific language models, physical AI, preemptive cybersecurity, digital provenance, AI security platforms, geopatriation.
- McKinsey Technology Trends Outlook 2025: AI as a foundational amplifier across technology categories, plus agentic AI, cloud/edge, digital trust, cybersecurity, and compute/connectivity.
- Coursera Job Skills Report 2026: enterprise learner demand across data, IT, software/product development, and generative AI skills.
- Google AI Search / AI Mode: search is moving from information retrieval toward AI-assisted reasoning, follow-up, and multimodal exploration.
- Microsoft small-business security guidance: AI-powered phishing and security risk are becoming practical SMB concerns.
- Zoom 2026 small-business tech trends: AI tools, cloud solutions, and AI-ready hardware are visible SMB priorities.
- CES / smart-home and wearable reporting: AI home robots, smart rings, smart glasses, AI smart home, and wearable health devices show consumer purchase interest.
- McKinsey AI search research: AI-powered search is already changing consumer discovery and purchase paths.

## Ranking Rules

- Prefer trends with 6-24 month or structural-shift lifespan.
- Prefer buyer intent: learn, buy, deploy, automate, secure, implement, audit, fix, train.
- Prefer IIS / ARIA / Growth Library fit.
- Penalize high legal, medical, investment, privacy, or unsupported claim risk.
- Avoid two-day memes unless they map to a durable behavior.
