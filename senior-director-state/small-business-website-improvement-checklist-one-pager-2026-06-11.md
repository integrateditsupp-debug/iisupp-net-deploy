# Small Business Website Improvement Checklist

Updated: 2026-06-11T02:10:00-04:00

## What it is
- A low-friction diagnostic product for owners who know the website feels weak but are not ready to hire for a full redesign.
- Built as a practical product entry that can stand alone or lead into a scoped IIS cleanup sprint.

## Best buyers
- Small business owners with a dated, unclear, or underperforming website
- Service businesses that rely on calls, quote requests, booking, or contact forms
- Buyers who want a practical checklist before paying for implementation help
- Website-service buyers who need a clearer self-audit before deciding what to fix first

## Problems it solves
- The site looks acceptable on the surface but does not create a clear next step
- Key trust, CTA, mobile, and intake issues are hard to spot internally
- Owners do not know whether the first fix is messaging, layout, contact flow, or follow-up
- Buyers need a simple self-review tool instead of a vague redesign pitch

## IIS outcome
- A practical checklist buyers can use to score clarity, trust, CTA, and intake friction
- A cleaner entry point into IIS website and intake cleanup work
- A safer product-to-service bridge for buyers who need implementation after the self-audit
- Clear separation between checklist guidance and any later scoped implementation commitment

## Safe scope language
- website clarity and offer-positioning self-review
- CTA and next-step friction checks
- contact, quote, booking, and intake-path review
- mobile trust and usability checks
- simple implementation-priority framing for the buyer's next decision

## Avoid claiming publicly without approval
- guaranteed lead or conversion uplift
- full redesign outcomes from the checklist alone
- AI/autonomous follow-up claims without human review
- fixed implementation pricing beyond a later scoped sprint posture
