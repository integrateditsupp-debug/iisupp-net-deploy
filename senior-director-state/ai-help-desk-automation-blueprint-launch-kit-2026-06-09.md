## Buyer
- MSP owner who needs to reduce repetitive L1 tickets without hiring immediately.
- IT manager with a small internal support team and messy escalation quality.
- Operations leader responsible for employee support but without a mature service desk.
- School, clinic, law firm, accounting firm, property group, or SMB that uses M365 and has repeated user-support pain.

## Problem
Support teams lose time because tickets arrive vague, repeated questions are answered manually, KB articles are inconsistent, and escalations lack context. AI can help, but most teams do not know how to structure it safely.

## Product promise
Build the operating blueprint for an AI-assisted help desk before buying tools or adding headcount. The pack gives the buyer triage logic, escalation templates, KB structure, pilot plan, and AI-readable support patterns.
