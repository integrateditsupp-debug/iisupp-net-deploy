## Recommended product ladder

### Rung 0 - Free

- `AI Readiness Scorecard`
- `Prompt Pack Generator`
- Goal:
  - capture email
  - segment buyer
  - point them to the right next product

Price:
- `Free`

### Rung 1 - Cheap info product

- `AI Edge Starter`
- Includes:
  - short practical lessons
  - prompt templates
  - AI workflow examples
  - one or two live sandboxes

Price:
- `US$29 to US$79 one-time`

Why:
- Cheap enough for impulse buy
- Expensive enough to avoid looking like junk

### Rung 2 - Better interactive product

- `AI Edge Pro Playbook`
- Includes:
  - all starter modules
  - more live sandboxes
  - saved outputs
  - business-specific templates
  - simple progress tracking

Price:
- `US$149 to US$299 one-time`

Why:
- This is where "advanced info costs more" starts showing clearly.
- It feels more like a tool than a course.

### Rung 3 - Recurring membership

- `AI Edge Membership`
- Includes:
  - updated playbooks
  - monthly templates
  - agent recipes
  - limited build-assistant usage
  - member-only implementation assets

Price:
- `US$99/month solo`
- `US$249/month team`
- `US$399/month business`

Why:
- Clear jump from self-serve learning into ongoing value.

### Rung 4 - High-ticket implementation

- `AI Agent Deployment Sprint`
- Includes:
  - discovery
  - one real workflow or agent
  - setup
  - handoff
  - optional support add-on

Price:
- `US$2,500 to US$7,500` for standard SMB scope
- `US$10,000 to US$15,000+` for larger or more complex deployments

### Rung 5 - Ongoing managed support

- `AI Operations Care Plan`
- Includes:
  - tuning
  - prompt cleanup
  - usage review
  - guardrails
  - change requests

Price:
- `US$500 to US$2,500/month`
