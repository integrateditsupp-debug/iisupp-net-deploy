# Integrated IT Support Inc. Capability Statement

Created: 2026-06-06

Status: internal public-safe draft. Do not publish, submit, or send externally until Ahmad approves wording, proof points, and any named references.

## Company Snapshot

Integrated IT Support Inc. provides senior-led IT support, Microsoft 365 readiness, workflow automation, website support, and practical AI adoption services for small and mid-sized organizations.

The company focuses on useful, controlled improvements: cleaner collaboration, better support workflows, safer AI readiness, stronger documentation, and technology decisions that business owners can understand.

## Core Services

### Microsoft 365 and AI Readiness

- Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, and Outlook workflow review.
- Permissions, collaboration, file structure, and governance readiness checks.
- AI readiness risk and opportunity mapping.
- 30/60/90 day roadmap for practical improvement.

### AI-Assisted Service Desk Starter

- Support intake workflow design.
- Ticket categories, first-response drafts, escalation rules, and knowledge-base starter.
- Human-supervised AI support workflow planning.
- Monthly improvement reporting model.

### Fractional IT Direction

- Senior IT advisory for businesses without a full-time IT leader.
- Vendor, license, project, support, and risk review.
- Monthly executive reporting and roadmap support.
- Coordination of specialist capacity when needed.

### Website Rescue and Care

- Website audit and conversion cleanup.
- Small business website refresh or landing page planning.
- Contact form, lead routing, and basic SEO structure.
- Ongoing care plan for updates and reliability.

### Workflow Automation and Documentation

- Intake forms, handoff workflows, SOPs, and internal knowledge-base support.
- Microsoft 365 workflow cleanup.
- Practical automation candidates and implementation planning.

## Differentiators

- Senior-led delivery rather than handoff-heavy support.
- Practical Microsoft 365, support, documentation, and AI workflow experience.
- Human-supervised AI adoption posture.
- Clear approval gates for security, legal, spend, and external commitments.
- Ability to coordinate partner or subcontractor capacity for larger/specialist work after scope approval.

## Target Buyers

- Accounting, bookkeeping, legal, clinic, nonprofit, construction, and professional-services teams.
- Owner-led businesses that rely on Microsoft 365 but do not have senior internal IT leadership.
- Organizations with repeated support questions, scattered documentation, weak intake workflows, or unclear AI readiness.
- Small vendors and operators that need practical technology cleanup before growth.

## Example Engagements

### M365 + AI Readiness Assessment

Outcome: A practical roadmap showing what to clean up before AI adoption, where workflow automation can help, and which Microsoft 365 risks or gaps should be addressed first.

Typical deliverables:
- Discovery summary.
- Microsoft 365 workflow findings.
- AI readiness risk/opportunity map.
- Quick wins.
- 30/60/90 day roadmap.

### AI Service Desk Starter

Outcome: A support workflow that makes repeated questions easier to intake, categorize, answer, escalate, and improve over time.

Typical deliverables:
- Intake structure.
- Knowledge-base starter.
- Response draft library.
- Escalation rules.
- Improvement report template.

### Fractional IT Director

Outcome: Better technology decisions, vendor coordination, and support oversight without hiring a full-time IT director.

Typical deliverables:
- Monthly leadership review.
- Vendor/license review.
- Roadmap and risk register.
- Executive report.
- Project prioritization.

## Public-Safe Proof Language

- Senior-led IT support and advisory.
- Experience across Microsoft 365, support workflows, documentation, AI-assisted support systems, and training.
- Practical, human-supervised AI adoption.
- Partner-backed capacity where specialized work is required.

Avoid until Ahmad approves evidence:
- Guaranteed savings or guaranteed outcomes.
- Named client claims.
- Certified SOC/ISO/security-monitoring claims.
- 24/7 global support claims.
- Large staff/bench claims.
