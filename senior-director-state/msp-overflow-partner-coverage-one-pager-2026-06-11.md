# MSP / Partner Overflow Coverage

Prepared: 2026-06-11
Owner: Codex
Status: no-send partner asset, ready for CEO final-action use

## What this is

Integrated IT Support can support MSPs, consultants, and project-led IT firms that need extra delivery capacity without turning a short-term crunch into a messy hiring or client-risk situation.

This is a partner-facing overflow support sheet, not a public claim of formal partnership. It is designed for quiet subcontract, white-label, or behind-the-scenes support conversations where the real value is capacity relief, better ticket hygiene, and cleaner handoff quality.

## Best-fit partner buyers

- MSP owners with backlog pressure or thin bench depth
- Boutique Microsoft 365, cloud, or infrastructure consultancies
- Project-led IT firms that need post-cutover support coverage
- Contractors or solution shops that need a safe overflow hand
- Regional providers covering vacation gaps, onboarding waves, or after-hours queue cleanup

## Partner pain

Small and mid-sized providers often hit the same friction point: there is enough work to need help, but not enough certainty to add full-time staff quickly. The result is ticket aging, weaker documentation, slower follow-up, and senior people getting dragged into repeatable issues they should not be handling themselves.

## IIS partner-side outcome

By the end of a scoped partner engagement, the provider should have:

- extra overflow capacity for agreed issue types
- cleaner triage and escalation-ready notes
- better queue visibility and daily status discipline
- support for Microsoft 365, onboarding/offboarding, and endpoint/user issues inside the agreed lane
- a clearer view of what can stay delegated, what needs senior escalation, and what should be automated or documented next

## Safe engagement models

- white-label overflow support under the partner's direction
- short backlog-burn-down sprint
- post-project hypercare support
- onboarding/offboarding or M365 admin assistance
- documentation and knowledge-base cleanup tied to support delivery

## What IIS can say confidently

- IIS can provide scoped overflow help around Microsoft 365, user support, onboarding/offboarding, documentation discipline, and escalation-quality improvement.
- IIS can work inside a tightly defined lane so the partner keeps control of client ownership, approval boundaries, and senior technical decisions.
- The value is practical capacity relief plus stronger support hygiene, not hype about scale.

## What IIS should not claim

- Do not claim formal partner status, reseller status, or OEM authorization unless Ahmad confirms it.
- Do not promise 24/7 coverage, guaranteed SLAs, or large bench depth without proof.
- Do not imply IIS will own the partner's client relationship unless the partner explicitly wants that model.
- Do not publish named partner logos, customer references, or protected delivery details without permission.

## Delivery guardrails

- Keep privileged access, production changes, and irreversible actions behind the partner's approval boundary.
- Keep the scope lane-specific: backlog reduction, user-support pressure, M365/admin issues, handoff cleanup, or hypercare.
- Preserve the partner's client relationship and escalation ownership unless a separate agreement says otherwise.

## Suggested starting conversation

Ask:

- where the queue pressure is showing up now
- which issue types are safe to delegate first
- whether the immediate need is backlog cleanup, M365/admin pressure, or post-project hypercare
- what approval boundary the partner wants to keep

Offer:

- one short overflow review call
- one anonymized queue/sample issue review
- one scoped pilot instead of an open-ended subcontract commitment
