Company Overview

Practical operational improvement, led by enterprise-tested delivery discipline

Integrated IT Support Inc. focuses on high-friction operational lanes where teams need reliable support readiness, cleaner workflows, stronger documentation, and practical Microsoft 365 or workplace technology help. IIS is founder-led and approval-gated by design, keeping delivery scoped, documented, and controlled.

  • Microsoft 365 support, cleanup, and readiness
  • Endpoint and user onboarding or offboarding readiness
  • Support runbooks, knowledge-base, and SOP delivery
  • Workflow automation and practical AI-assisted operations
  • Scoped workplace technology and office-readiness support
