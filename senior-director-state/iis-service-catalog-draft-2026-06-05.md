## 1. M365 + AI Readiness Assessment

Buyer: small and mid-sized companies using Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Outlook, or Copilot/Copilot Chat.

Problem: companies want AI but their permissions, files, SharePoint sites, Teams sprawl, and governance are not ready.

Deliverables:
- Discovery session.
- M365 tenant and workflow review checklist.
- SharePoint/Teams/OneDrive structure review.
- AI readiness risk list.
- Automation opportunity map.
- 30/60/90 day roadmap.
- Executive summary.

Draft price:
- CAD/USD 3,500 to 7,500 fixed fee.

Gross margin target:
- 70%+ if Ahmad-led.

Upsell:
- Monthly AI/M365 governance support.
- SharePoint cleanup.
- AI service desk starter.
