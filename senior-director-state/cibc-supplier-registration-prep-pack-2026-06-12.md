## What this is

This packet prepares Integrated IT Support Inc. for CIBC's official supplier-intake path without sending the intake email, attaching any file externally, or making any legal/compliance representation beyond staged draft language.

## Public entry point tracked

- Becoming a CIBC Supplier: https://www.cibc.com/en/about-cibc/sustainability/to-our-suppliers/becoming-a-cibc-supplier.html

## Public page verified on 2026-06-12

- CIBC publicly accepts prospective supplier information by email at `BecomeaSupplier@cibc.com`.
- CIBC publicly asks that the intake email contain all of the following:
  - legal company name
  - operating name
  - number of employees
  - brief overview of the company
  - what makes the company unique
  - brief overview of the product or service offering
  - years in business, including date of incorporation or registration
  - diverse supplier classifications, if applicable
  - company website
  - social media sites, if applicable
  - primary contact first and last name
  - primary contact email address
  - mailing address
  - city
  - province or state
  - country
  - DUNS code, if applicable
  - NAICS and SIC codes
  - capabilities sheet as a PDF that includes at least one case study where the client is similar in size or scope as CIBC's
- CIBC also states it is not obligated to use the supplier's services and will contact only suitable potential suppliers when sourcing activities arise.

## Why this lane is worth preparing

- CIBC provides a real, no-cost official intake path instead of a closed registration gate.
- IIS can truthfully position around:
  - Microsoft 365 support and cleanup
  - workflow automation and practical AI-assisted operations
  - endpoint and user readiness
  - support-runbook, SOP, and documentation work
  - scoped workplace technology services
- The lane is worth preparing even if the first email is not sent immediately because the required field list is explicit and reusable across other enterprise supplier-entry lanes.
