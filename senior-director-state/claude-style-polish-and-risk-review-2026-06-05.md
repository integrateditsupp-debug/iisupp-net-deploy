# Claude-Style Polish and Risk Review

Created: 2026-06-05

Purpose: satisfy the queued Claude Code review task using a professional-bidder lens: polish the company improvement board and service catalog while flagging overclaiming risk.

## Executive Verdict

The IIS positioning is strong if kept narrow and credible:

> Senior-led Microsoft 365, AI readiness, support workflow, and practical automation help for small and mid-sized organizations.

This is better than presenting IIS as a large MSP, enterprise AI platform vendor, cybersecurity SOC, or global staffing company. The win is confidence without inflation.

## Best Public Positioning

Use this:

Integrated IT Support Inc. helps small and mid-sized organizations improve Microsoft 365, support workflows, SharePoint/Teams collaboration, documentation, and practical AI readiness. IIS is senior-led by Ahmad Wasee and can coordinate qualified specialist capacity when a project requires it, while keeping scope, quality, and escalation under accountable IIS direction.

## Strongest Offers

### 1. M365 + AI Readiness Assessment

Why it is strong:
- Fits Ahmad's actual background.
- Easy to explain.
- Low delivery risk.
- Opens monthly support, SharePoint cleanup, security baseline, and AI service desk work.

Polished wording:

Before adopting AI across the business, IIS helps clients understand whether Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, permissions, support workflows, and staff guidance are ready for safe and useful AI adoption.

### 2. AI Service Desk Starter

Why it is strong:
- Connects ARIA/internal project work to a client-safe business use case.
- Avoids the trap of claiming a full enterprise AI platform.

Polished wording:

IIS helps clients structure a human-supervised AI support workflow: intake, triage, knowledge base, first-response drafts, escalation rules, and monthly improvement reporting.

### 3. Fractional IT Director

Why it is strong:
- Directly monetizes Ahmad's senior experience.
- Creates recurring revenue.
- Does not require pretending to have a large help desk.

Polished wording:

IIS provides practical senior IT guidance for organizations that need better vendor control, Microsoft 365 direction, project prioritization, AI adoption planning, and support oversight without hiring a full-time IT director.

## Overclaiming Risks To Remove

Avoid these unless Ahmad approves proof:

- "24/7 support."
- "Global support team."
- "Certified SOC / ISO / MDR."
- "Guaranteed savings."
- "Fully autonomous AI."
- "Enterprise AI platform delivery."
- "We support all technologies."
- "Large bench."
- Named client claims without permission.
- Security/compliance claims that sound like audit certification.

## Better Language Replacements

| Risky Wording | Safer Wording |
|---|---|
| Large global team | Senior-led delivery with controlled specialist capacity |
| 24/7 support | Support hours defined by the service agreement |
| Fully automated AI support | Human-supervised AI-assisted support workflow |
| Compliance-ready | Designed with practical governance and risk awareness |
| Certified cybersecurity service | Security baseline review and partner-backed specialist support where required |
| Enterprise AI implementation | AI readiness, workflow automation, and support enablement |
| Unlimited support | Defined monthly support scope with clear escalation |

## Tender Response Language

Use:

IIS will assign qualified resources based on the final statement of work, security requirements, location requirements, and client approval process. Ahmad Wasee remains the accountable senior lead for delivery governance, scope control, quality, escalation, and client communication.

Avoid:

IIS has all resources ready for every role.

## Website Copy Recommendations

Homepage should lead with one clear sentence:

> Practical IT support, Microsoft 365 readiness, and AI workflow help for growing businesses.

CTA:

> Book a readiness review.

Secondary CTA:

> Ask about support packages.

Do not publish pricing immediately. Use:

> Fixed-scope assessments and monthly support packages are available after a short discovery review.

## Case Study Structure

Use anonymized case-study style until Ahmad approves names:

Focus:

- Documentation.

Avoid:

### ARIA Internal AI Support Project

Focus:

Avoid:

## Proposal Tone Standard

The tone should be:

- Confident.
- Specific.
- Senior-led.
- Evidence-backed.
- Practical.
- No hype.

Not:

- Desperate.
- Grandiose.
- Vague.
- Certification-heavy without proof.
- Large-vendor imitation.
