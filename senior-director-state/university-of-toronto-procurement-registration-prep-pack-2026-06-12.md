## Publicly verified on 2026-06-13

- The Prospective Suppliers page says suppliers become Approved Suppliers by:
  - responding to open competitive procurement opportunities posted on MERX or Biddingo and meeting evaluation criteria, or
  - holding a valid agreement through listed GPO paths such as OECM, Supply Ontario, KineticGPO, or Canoe Procurement Group of Canada.
- The same page says suppliers interested in marketing goods or services should contact relevant faculties or departments directly for purchases below CAD 121,200 and monitor MERX/Biddingo for purchases at or above that threshold.
- The Doing Business contact form is explicitly for general inquiries and says it is not used to schedule supplier meetings or introductory appointments.
- The New Supplier Account Request page says the form is used to set up a new or reactivate an existing vendor account in FIS and links to a SharePoint `Supplier Request Form`.
- Opening the linked SharePoint `Supplier Request Form` from the public page redirects to Microsoft sign-in, which is a strong signal that the actual supplier-account request document is not a clean external self-registration flow.

## What is safe to assume from the public pages

- U of T has formal procurement infrastructure rather than an informal contact-only process.
- U of T uses approved-supplier, MERX/Biddingo, Bonfire, and uSOURCE concepts, which suggests many purchases route through established vendor records, public solicitations, or internal buyer-controlled requests.
- The public pages do not make it safe to assume that an external supplier can freely self-register directly into U of T's supplier master without an internal requester, awarded opportunity, or controlled procurement path.
