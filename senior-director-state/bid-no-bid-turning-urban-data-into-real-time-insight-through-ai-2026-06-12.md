## Why It Is Not A Clean Direct IIS Bid

- The challenge asks for AI-driven solutions that repurpose urban infrastructure into a distributed sensing network for real-time situational awareness and anomaly detection.
- Canada states that DND/CAF will not provide training, fine-tuning, validation, or representative data.
- The notice says participation assumes innovators already have enough sensor-domain expertise to generate or obtain suitable datasets independently.
- Essential outcomes include multi-source real-time ingestion, anomaly detection, and machine-readable outputs, which is much closer to an R&D / advanced product challenge than a standard IIS services engagement.
- The notice explicitly frames this as building sovereign Canadian capacity and IP in ambient urban intelligence, which raises the technical bar beyond current IIS direct-delivery proof.

## Official Facts Confirmed In This Run

From the official CanadaBuys notice:

- The challenge is under the `Innovation for Defence Excellence and Security (IDEaS) Program Call for Proposals 006`.
- Proposals can be submitted across `TRL 1-9`.
- Maximum funding is up to:
  - `CAD 250,000` for Component 1a
  - `CAD 1,500,000` for Component 1b
  - `CAD 5,000,000` for Component 2
- Canada says the solution should ingest data from at least two heterogeneous sources and produce machine-readable alerts/scores/flags.
- The examples given include unauthorized drone detection, critical-infrastructure anomaly detection, and environmental early warning.
