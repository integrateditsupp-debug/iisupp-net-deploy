## First Three Offers To Package

### 1. M365 + AI Readiness Assessment

Sell as the front-door offer.

Recommended starter price:
- CAD 4,500 for up to 25 users.
- CAD 6,500 for 26 to 75 users.
- Custom above 75 users.

Delivery estimate:
- 12 to 20 Ahmad hours.
- 1 to 2 weeks elapsed.

Margin logic:
- Mostly discovery, analysis, documentation, roadmap, and executive summary.
- Can be delivered before building heavy infrastructure.

Upsells:
- M365 Security Baseline.
- AI Service Desk Starter.
- SharePoint cleanup.
- Monthly governance retainer.

### 2. AI Service Desk Starter

Sell after readiness or to businesses with obvious support pain.

Recommended starter price:
- CAD 7,500 basic setup.
- CAD 12,500 standard setup.
- CAD 2,000 monthly tuning and reporting.

Delivery estimate:
- 20 to 45 hours depending on workflow complexity.
- 2 to 4 weeks elapsed.

Margin logic:
- Reusable intake, triage, KB, response, and escalation templates.
- ARIA story gives credibility, but avoid claiming ARIA is fully client-ready until scoped.

Upsells:
- Monthly help desk.
- Knowledge base expansion.
- M365 security and compliance review.

### 3. Fractional IT Director

Sell as recurring trust builder.

Recommended starter price:
- CAD 2,500 per month basic advisory.
- CAD 5,000 per month standard advisory plus reporting.
- CAD 8,000 per month high-touch support and project governance.

Delivery estimate:
- 4 to 16 Ahmad hours per month.

Margin logic:
- Advisory and governance-led.
- Creates steady income and opens follow-on projects.

Upsells:
- Implementation projects.
- M365 + AI readiness.
- Support retainer.
- Vendor management.

## Pricing Rules

- Do not discount the first offer below CAD 3,500 unless it is a strategic reference.
- Avoid hourly-only pricing for advisory packages.
- Use fixed-scope language and change-control language.
- Keep monthly retainers simple: Basic, Standard, High-Touch.
- Any 24/7, SOC/MDR, cybersecurity monitoring, onsite, or global coverage claim must be partner-backed and approved.

## Margin Guardrails

- Target 70%+ margin on Ahmad-led advisory.
- Target 60%+ margin on website work after subcontractor cost.
- Target 45% to 65% margin on offshore support until QA cost is known.
- Do not accept fixed-price implementation work without a clear scope boundary.
- Do not sell compliance certifications unless the evidence or partner is already in place.
