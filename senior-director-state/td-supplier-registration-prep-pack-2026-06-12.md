## What this is

This packet prepares Integrated IT Support Inc. for the TD supplier-access lane tied to TD's public supplier page without creating an account, certifying anything, or submitting vendor data.

## Public entry points tracked

- TD supplier page: https://www.td.com/ca/en/about-td/supplier-information
- TD supplier and vendor sourcing information: https://www.td.com/us/en/personal-banking/suppliers

## What is safe to assume from the public entry point

- TD publicly presents this as a supplier-request lane first, not direct full registration into active transacting status.
- The first public step is a supplier request through SAP Ariba.
- Full supplier registration comes later only if TD selects the supplier through a sourcing event.

## Live path verified on 2026-06-13

- TD's current Canadian supplier page is `https://www.td.com/ca/en/about-td/supplier-information`; the older `for-suppliers` path resolves to `404`.
- TD's public supplier page says the first step is to submit a supplier request through SAP Ariba.
- TD says the supplier request is not a commitment to do business; it only places the company into TD's procurement database for possible sourcing-event consideration.
- TD publicly lists the minimum company information expected at the request stage:
  - legal company name
  - legal address
  - contact information
  - relationship to TD, if any
- TD says the supplier request stage requires the supplier to certify it has adopted guidelines, policies, and/or practices that operate in accordance with TD's Supplier Code of Conduct.
- TD says only suppliers selected through a sourcing event later receive an email invitation to register with TD through SAP Ariba and complete the Supplier Registration Questionnaire.
- TD also says later due diligence can include business and technology viability reviews covering policies and procedures, vendor financial health, and physical and cyber security controls.
- TD states that SAP Ariba fees associated with TD transactions are covered by TD, though other customers or optional Ariba services can still create fees outside the TD relationship.
- The live `Go to request form` link currently resolves to `http://td.supplier.ariba.com/ad/selfRegistration`, then redirects into SAP Ariba at `s1.ariba.com`.
- The live SAP Ariba request surface is currently gated by reCAPTCHA before the supplier fields become visible, so the exact form layout could not be inspected without Ahmad solving CAPTCHA.
- Final CEO checklist for the live gate: `senior-director-state/td-supplier-register-checklist-2026-06-13.md`
