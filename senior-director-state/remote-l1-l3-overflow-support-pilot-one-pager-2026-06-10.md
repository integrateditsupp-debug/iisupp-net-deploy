## Offer

**Remote L1-L3 Overflow Support Pilot**

Integrated IT Support helps busy IT teams, MSPs, and operations-heavy businesses take pressure off day-to-day support without forcing a long outsourcing commitment or a risky platform change.

This is a short pilot designed to absorb support load, improve escalation hygiene, and give the buyer a clearer view of what should stay in-house versus what can be delegated safely.

## Buyer

- MSP owner with ticket backlog, thin coverage, or inconsistent documentation
- IT manager with a small internal team covering too many users and systems
- Operations leader who owns employee support without a mature help desk structure
- Growing business with Microsoft 365, endpoint, onboarding, and user-support pressure
- Project or move-in team that needs temporary day-one or post-change support coverage

## Problem

Teams lose time when straightforward support work keeps interrupting senior staff, tickets arrive without context, repeated user issues are handled inconsistently, and nobody has time to tighten the workflow while still keeping the queue moving.

## Pilot Outcome

By the end of the pilot, the buyer has:

- active overflow support coverage for agreed issue types
- cleaner triage and ticket categorization
- stronger L1/L2/L3 escalation notes
- recurring-issue visibility
- daily owner summary with open-risk view
- support workflow observations and cleanup recommendations
- shortlist of repeatable tasks IIS can continue or automate

## Suggested Price Posture

- Short discovery / queue review: **$250-$750**
- Two-week overflow pilot: **$1,500-$4,000**
- Ongoing weekly or monthly support coverage: **custom after pilot**

Do not publish final pricing without Ahmad approval.

## Suggested Pilot Scope

- Ticket triage and first-response structure
- Microsoft 365 user support
- Onboarding / offboarding support steps
- Endpoint and access issue handling
- Support-note cleanup and escalation handoff
- Daily summary to the owner or IT lead

## Guardrails

- Keep privileged access, production changes, and irreversible actions under client approval.
- Use the pilot to absorb routine pressure and improve handoff quality, not to promise full managed-services replacement.
- Exclude unsupported claims around staffing depth, certifications, or 24/7 coverage unless Ahmad confirms them.

## Delivery Steps

1. Short intake and support-pressure review
2. Confirm ticket types, tools, hours, and approval boundaries
3. Review sample queue or common issue list
4. Start controlled overflow coverage for the agreed scope
5. Track repeated issues, escalation gaps, and documentation friction
6. Send daily owner summary with status, risks, and observations
7. Deliver end-of-pilot findings with continue / stop / automate recommendations

## What IIS Can Say Confidently

- IIS can help reduce day-to-day support pressure through scoped overflow coverage, Microsoft 365/user support, documentation discipline, and escalation-quality improvement.
- The offer is intentionally pilot-first so the buyer can test fit before expanding scope.
- The value is practical coverage plus better visibility, not hype or false scale claims.

## What IIS Should Not Claim Without Proof

- Do not claim 24/7 coverage.
- Do not claim large bench depth, NOC/SOC staffing, or enterprise SLA guarantees unless Ahmad confirms them.
- Do not claim named client results, percentage improvements, or regulated-environment approvals without evidence.
- Do not imply IIS is replacing the buyer's full IT function in the pilot stage.
