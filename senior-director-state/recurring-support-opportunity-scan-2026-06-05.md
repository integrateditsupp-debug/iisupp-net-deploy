## Direct Sales Targets To Build

The best recurring support lane is direct SMB sales, not only tender portals.

Target categories:

1. Accounting/bookkeeping firms using Microsoft 365.
2. Small legal offices with document and SharePoint pain.
3. Clinics with repeated staff support/intake questions.
4. Nonprofits with no internal IT leader.
5. Construction/trades admin offices with file/version chaos.
6. Small MSPs needing overflow help.
7. Real estate/property management offices.
8. Training/tutoring/coaching businesses with repeated learner support.
9. Local ecommerce/retail operations needing website and M365 cleanup.
10. Professional associations with committees, files, events, and member support.

Find 10 live recurring support opportunities only if:
