## Executive Recommendation

IIS should sell AI as practical workflow improvement, not as abstract "enterprise AI transformation." The easiest buyers are organizations with repeated questions, repeated document handling, repeated intake, repeated reporting, or repeated support tasks.

Lead offer:
- M365 + AI Readiness Assessment.

Follow-on offers:
- AI Service Desk Starter.
- SharePoint/knowledge-base cleanup.
- Workflow automation sprint.
- Monthly AI governance and improvement retainer.

## Best Initial Vertical Targets

### Professional Services

Examples:
- Accounting firms.
- Legal offices.
- Consulting firms.
- Mortgage/financial advisory offices.

Why:
- Heavy document/email workload.
- Sensitive information means AI readiness matters.
- Microsoft 365 is common.
- Decision maker often sees the pain directly.

### Healthcare / Clinics

Examples:
- Dental, physio, wellness, specialist clinics.

Why:
- Repeated intake and support questions.
- Staff technology friction is visible.
- Needs careful privacy posture.

Guardrail:
- Do not claim HIPAA/PHIPA compliance services unless scoped and reviewed.

### Nonprofits

Why:
- Small teams, limited IT leadership, repeated support/admin work.
- Good fit for fractional IT director and AI support desk starter.

### Construction / Trades Admin

Why:
- Lots of quotes, documents, photos, field coordination, onboarding, and vendor/admin workflows.
- Website and M365 cleanup can both sell.

### Small MSPs

Why:
- They understand support value.
- They may buy white-label help, proposal support, or AI workflow setup.

Guardrail:
- Do not compete directly with a prospect's client base during partnership talks.

## First Offer Path

1. Sell M365 + AI Readiness Assessment.
2. Deliver roadmap and quick-win list.
3. Convert quick wins into:
   - SharePoint cleanup.
   - AI service desk starter.
   - Security baseline.
   - Monthly fractional IT director.

## No-Go AI Claims

Do not claim:
- Guaranteed ROI.
- Fully autonomous support.
- Compliance certification.
- Replacement of human IT staff.
- Secure use of client data in AI tools without written approval and technical review.
- Enterprise AI platform delivery unless scoped and partner-backed.
