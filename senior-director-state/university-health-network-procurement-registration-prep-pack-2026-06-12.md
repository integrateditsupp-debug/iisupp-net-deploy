## Live path verified on 2026-06-13

- UHN's official business-opportunity page says procurement opportunities and award notices should be searched on `Biddingo.com`.
- The same page says UHN also uses `Bonfire` as an electronic vendor response and evaluation tool.
- UHN publicly lists `purchasing@uhn.ca` for further procurement information.
- UHN's Ariba page says UHN's preferred supplier-transaction method is the SAP Business Network.
- The Ariba page also says suppliers are invited to join in waves and UHN will notify suppliers when it wants them to start transacting through the network.
- UHN publicly lists `aribasupplierenablement@uhn.ca` only for questions regarding supplier onboarding to the SAP Business Network.

## What is safe to assume from the public pages

- UHN separates visitor/vendor-access rules from procurement/business-opportunity processes.
- Procurement opportunities are publicly tied to Biddingo and Bonfire rather than a broad open self-registration page.
- UHN publishes a procurement contact path and a separate Ariba enablement path, but the public pages do not make it safe to assume that IIS should email the Ariba team before UHN has invited it into onboarding.
- The visitor-vendor forms still should not be treated as the procurement path for IIS.
