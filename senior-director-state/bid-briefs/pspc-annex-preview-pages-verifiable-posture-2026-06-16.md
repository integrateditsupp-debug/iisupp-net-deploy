## Why this annex exists

The Government of Canada Directive on Automated Decision-Making and the Algorithmic Impact Assessment process require AI vendors to operate under documented governance. Many bidders make those claims in their submissions; few have them codified on public-facing surfaces.

This annex points evaluators at Integrated IT Support's public product-preview library — 15 sample-preview pages, each carrying an explicit "what we are not claiming" section. The library was built before this submission. The posture is operational, not bid-time positioning.

---

## How to use this annex (for the evaluator)

Open any URL below. Locate the section labeled "what stayed safe" or its equivalent. Each page documents what IIS will NOT promise as part of that product. Examples encountered when this annex was prepared:

- Anti-autonomy claims: "no false autonomous-send claims, no hidden data-movement promises"
- Anti-overclaim on coverage: "No 24/7 coverage or hard SLA promises without proof"
- Anti-overclaim on scope: "It is built to create better employee judgment, not to pretend a short guide replaces a full security program"
- Anti-overclaim on impact: "without public pricing, guaranteed time-saved claims, deep integration promises, or fake autonomous-operation posture"

These appear on every preview page in the library. Spot-check any URL to verify.
