## Tone choices made (reasoning preserved for future edits)

- **Opens with credibility, not flattery.** Federal evaluators see hundreds of "honored to submit" letters. Substance in paragraph 1 wins more attention.
- **Names the three differentiators by number** so the evaluator's cognitive load is low.
- **"Methodology-led" + "scoped"** signals IIS isn't trying to bundle. Evaluators trust bidders who limit themselves.
- **"No claims it could"** about ARIA — anti-claim language, aligns with federal evaluator skepticism about AI vendors.
- **Closes with direct contact**, not procurement bureaucracy — small federal vendors win on responsiveness.
