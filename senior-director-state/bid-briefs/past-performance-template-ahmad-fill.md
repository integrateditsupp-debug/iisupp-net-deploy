# Past-Performance Template — Ahmad Fill Once, Cowork Reuses Forever

**Why this exists:** Past-performance evidence is the **single biggest blocker** across every federal bid (OSFI, PSPC, DND, Sourcewell, future). Evaluators reject responses without 3 documented prior engagements no matter how strong the methodology is. Ahmad has the history — Cowork doesn't. One 15-min fill of this file unlocks every bid.

**Time to fill:** ~15 minutes for 3 engagements.
**Reuse:** Cowork pulls relevant rows into every future bid response section 5. Zero further Ahmad time per bid.
**Update cadence:** add a new row whenever a project wraps that's defensible as a reference.

## How Cowork uses this

Every federal bid response section 5 needs 1–3 engagements selected by relevance:

- **OSFI Ransomware Tabletop** → pick any engagement with `Federal-bid relevance flags: Cybersecurity` OR `Workflow`. If none, use methodology-only bid (weaker, still viable).
- **PSPC AI Source List** → pick engagements with `Federal-bid relevance flags: AI implementation`. Even one strong AI implementation qualifies IIS for Band 1.
- **Generic L1-L3 / managed support** → pick `Managed support`.
- **Website rescue bids** → pick `Web`.

Cowork can also draft a "scope/outcomes" rewrite per bid evaluator's language preference without changing the underlying facts. Ahmad confirms the rewrite.

---

## What counts as a "qualifying" engagement

Federal evaluators are reasonable about what they accept. **All of these qualify:**

- A paid project with a named client (best).
- A paid project with an anonymized client (acceptable; reveal under NDA at evaluator request).
- An ongoing retainer (counts — fill "Engagement period: Ongoing since YYYY-MM").
- A pilot, MVP, or proof-of-concept that delivered measurable outcome (counts).
- IIS internal work on iisupp.net + ARIA platform development (counts as "internal R&D" engagement — value = IIS's own investment).
- A subcontracted role under a prime contractor (counts — name the prime if permission, anonymized otherwise).

**What does NOT count:**

- Hypothetical or planned work.
- Volunteer / pro bono unless explicitly delivering enterprise outcomes.
- Resold software with no IIS-delivered services.

## Confidentiality posture

- Leaving `Client name` blank or `[CONFIDENTIAL]` is fine — flag it under `Reference contact permission: Anonymized only`. Federal evaluators accept this in initial response and may ask for reveal in due diligence (under NDA).
- IIS does NOT make claims that can't be backed up under evaluator scrutiny. False past-performance is an immediate disqualifier in every federal procurement system.

## Stop rules

- Do not invent engagements.
- Do not list a client without their permission to name OR `Anonymized only` posture.
- Do not list a reference contact without that contact's prior confirmation they'll take a call.
- Do not commit to a value Ahmad can't substantiate from invoices/contracts.
