# IIS Company Improvement Board

Created: 2026-06-05

Purpose: improve Integrated IT Support Inc. as an operating company, not only chase bids. This board converts current market signals, IIS strengths, and agent capacity into executable work.

## Operating Thesis

IIS should position as a senior-led M365, AI readiness, IT support, and workflow automation company. The fastest money is packaged advisory and recurring support. Tenders and global contracts are still useful, but only when they fit the evidence and do not require impossible certifications, local staffing, bonds, or unsupported legal claims.

Market signal:
- SMBs are adopting AI faster than they are building governance, training, and operating strategy.
- MSP buyers are asking for AI, automation, cybersecurity, M365/Copilot readiness, and managed services.
- Many organizations have M365 already, but lack clean SharePoint, Teams, Entra, Intune, data, and support workflows.

IIS advantage:
- Ahmad has real support, AI, M365, SharePoint, ITSM, tutoring/training, and financial-services experience.
- ARIA and the knowledge-base assets create a credible AI service desk story.
- The company can be senior-led with a vetted subcontractor/partner bench for surge roles.
