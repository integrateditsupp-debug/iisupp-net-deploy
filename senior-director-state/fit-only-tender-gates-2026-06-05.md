# Fit-Only Tender Gates

Created: 2026-06-05

Owner: tender-review-agent

Status: completed draft. Use before any portal upload, declaration, pricing, or signature.

## Universal Auto-Reject Gates

Reject or partner-only if any of these are mandatory at proposal time:

- Defence, classified, protected/military operational dependency, or security clearance IIS does not hold.
- SOC 2, ISO 27001, cyber certification, auditor letter, or MDR/SOC proof IIS cannot provide or partner-back.
- Bid bond, performance bond, surety, liquidated damages, or penalty terms Ahmad has not approved.
- Mandatory local onsite staffing IIS cannot provide.
- Mandatory municipal/public-sector references IIS cannot truthfully satisfy.
- Reseller/CSP/OEM partner-only requirement without the partner path already approved.
- Insurance limit, indemnity, or legal declaration not reviewed by Ahmad.
- Requirement to fabricate bench size, certifications, client names, or prior contracts.

## 1. Invest Nova Scotia Standing Offer

Source: https://investnovascotia.ca/invest-nova-scotia-standing-offer

- IT consulting.
- AI readiness and workflow automation.
- M365/SharePoint/Teams support.

- Reporting and analytics.
- Training and project support.

- Confirm whether subcontractor/partner disclosure is required.

Bid/no-bid decision:
- Bid if categories are broad professional services and resume-backed.
- Hold if it demands certifications, named public-sector references, or insurance Ahmad cannot confirm.
