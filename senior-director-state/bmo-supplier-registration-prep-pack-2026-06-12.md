## What this is

This packet prepares Integrated IT Support Inc. for the BMO supplier-access lane without creating an account, certifying anything, or submitting vendor data.

## Public entry points tracked

- Current BMO Canada supplier information page: https://www.bmo.com/en-ca/main/about-bmo/who-we-are/business-conduct/supplier-info/
- Current BMO Canada business-site nav target for `Register as a diverse supplier`: https://www.bmo.com/main/about-bmo/corporate-information/current-supplier-information
- Older public supplier-diversity URL now returns `404 Not Found`: https://corporate-responsibility.bmo.com/our-practices/supplier-diversity/

## What is safe to assume from the public entry point

- BMO does not present this as a broad public self-registration lane.
- BMO says a BMO Financial Group contact must submit an internal request to set up a supplier profile in Coupa before a company can transact with BMO.
- BMO publicly exposes a procurement helpdesk for requirement questions, but the helpdesk is not the same as supplier approval.

## Live path verified on 2026-06-13

- BMO's current Canada business-site `Supplier diversity / Register as a diverse supplier` navigation does not expose a separate public diversity intake form. It resolves into BMO's supplier information page.
- BMO's current supplier information page says the Coupa Supplier Portal is used for supplier self-service after onboarding, but the company profile must first be set up through an internal request from a BMO contact.
- BMO publicly lists `Procurement.HelpDesk@bmo.com` for supplier requirement questions.
- BMO publishes the minimum transactional posture it expects once a supplier is onboarded:
  - invoicing through Coupa Supplier Portal
  - payment terms of 45 days from invoice receipt
  - payment by EFT/ACH
- BMO also lists onboarding documents that can be required once a supplier is moving forward:
  - ACH/DEFT form
  - void cheque or bank letter
  - tax documentation
  - W9 for US vendors
  - W8 for suppliers outside the US doing business with a US entity
- BMO's supplier resources/governance posture says third-party onboarding can involve a risk assessment and due-diligence review covering items such as policies and procedures, privacy, cyber and technology security controls, regulatory expectations, and financial health.
- BMO's supplier resources/governance posture also says suppliers must inform BMO when AI functionality, including generative AI, is added to or changed within services for the bank and obtain BMO consent before using generative AI to create deliverables or deliver services.
- The older public supplier-diversity URL now returns `404 Not Found`, so it should no longer be treated as the live entry point.
