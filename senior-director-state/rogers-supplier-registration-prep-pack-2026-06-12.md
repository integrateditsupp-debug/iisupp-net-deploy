## What this is

This packet prepares Integrated IT Support Inc. for the Rogers supplier-portal path without creating an account, registering, or accepting any supplier-code or legal attestation.

## Public entry points tracked

- Rogers supplier portal: https://supplierportal.rogers.com/
- Contact page: https://supplierportal.rogers.com/contact_us.html
- Supplier code of conduct: https://supplierportal.rogers.com/Code_of_Conduct.html
- Ivalua supplier login/register page: https://rogers-suppliers.ivalua.app/page.aspx/en/usr/login?ReturnUrl=%2Fpage.aspx%2Fen%2Fbuy%2Fhomepage

## What is safe to assume from the public pages

- Rogers has a public supplier portal with a visible self-registration/login surface.
- Rogers publishes procurement support contacts on the portal, including `Procurement@rci.rogers.com` for general assistance.
- Rogers publicly posts a supplier code of conduct, which means downstream registration may involve ethics, labour, environmental, privacy, or compliance acknowledgements.

## Live path verified on 2026-06-12

- The public Rogers supplier portal links directly to an Ivalua login/register page.
- The Ivalua page says `New Supplier? Register Now` is intended only for companies that are not already existing Rogers suppliers.
- The live register path is gated by a browser-check CAPTCHA before the self-registration form appears.
- The Ivalua page states that registration does not obligate Rogers to buy from IIS and is only for inclusion in the supplier database and possible future RFx eligibility.
- The portal also exposes two separate support paths:
  - `Procurement@rci.rogers.com` for general procurement support.
  - `RogersIvaluaSupport@rci.rogers.com` if an existing Rogers supplier needs login credentials.
- Ivalua helpdesk details are publicly visible on the login page:
  - `support-rogers@ivalua.com`
  - US number: `+1 703 344 71 92`
- The contact page also publishes the exact fields Rogers wants from an existing vendor requesting invoice-status portal access:
  - supplier name
  - email
  - first name
  - last name
  - phone number
  - GST/HST/Tax ID
  - city
  - province/state
  - postal/zip code
  - country
- On 2026-06-13 the live `Register Now` route was rechecked in-browser and again resolved to `https://rogers-suppliers.ivalua.app/page.aspx/en/bas/browser_check` with the explicit message `Please solve this captcha in order to continue.`
- Because the CAPTCHA remained unsolved, the actual self-registration field layout could not be inspected further without Ahmad's direct intervention.
