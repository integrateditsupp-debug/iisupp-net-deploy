## What is safe to assume from the public pages

- TELUS has a formal procurement function with publicly visible supplier-related policy pages.
- TELUS highlights supplier diversity and supplier sustainability, which suggests that supplier-code, ESG, and diversity topics may matter in downstream onboarding.
- TELUS publicly offers a direct `Become/Register to be a TELUS supplier` path into SAP Ariba.

## Live path verified on 2026-06-12

- TELUS procurement explicitly says supplier registration is free and required to become a TELUS supplier.
- TELUS says registration alone does not place a company on a bidder list, does not equal supplier approval, and does not obligate TELUS to solicit quotes.
- TELUS says supplier information remains in its confidential supplier database for 12 months, or longer if the supplier keeps its information updated through the procurement site.
- TELUS publishes SAP Ariba helpdesk numbers for registration/login support on the procurement page.
- TELUS also publishes an additional supplier due-diligence layer:
- qualified suppliers may be invited to register with Avetta for pre-qualification and risk assessment
  - suppliers are responsible for any costs associated with Avetta registration
- TELUS's 2025 Supplier Code of Conduct explicitly covers anti-bribery, privacy, information security, cybersecurity, intellectual property, fair business practices, conflicts, and responsible AI.
- The code specifically says suppliers must disclose relevant AI use, avoid using TELUS data for AI training unless expressly permitted, and support TELUS's own responsible-AI obligations.
- On 2026-06-13 the live `Become a TELUS supplier` link resolved into the Ariba registration page at `https://service.ariba.com/Register.aw/...` without a CAPTCHA.
- The live Ariba form visibly exposed company-information and user-account fields before the final register step.
