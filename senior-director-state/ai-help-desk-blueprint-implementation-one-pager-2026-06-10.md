## Offer

**AI Help Desk Blueprint Implementation**

Integrated IT Support helps IT teams, MSPs, and growing businesses design a practical AI-assisted help desk flow for ticket intake, triage, escalation quality, repeated questions, and knowledge base reuse.

This is not a generic AI chatbot pitch. It is a structured implementation engagement for support operations.

## Buyer

- MSP owner with repetitive L1 tickets and inconsistent handoffs
- IT manager with a small team and weak ticket context
- Operations leader responsible for staff support but without mature service desk structure
- School, clinic, law firm, accounting firm, property group, or SMB using Microsoft 365

## Problem

Support teams lose time because tickets arrive vague, common questions are answered manually, escalations miss context, and knowledge articles are inconsistent. AI can help, but only if the intake, triage, KB, and escalation workflow are designed properly first.

## Outcome

By the end of the engagement, the buyer has:

- support pain audit
- ticket intake model
- L1/L2/L3 triage matrix
- escalation note template
- ticket summary template
- repeated-question deflection map
- AI-readable KB article structure
- pilot rollout checklist
- QA checklist
- practical implementation roadmap
