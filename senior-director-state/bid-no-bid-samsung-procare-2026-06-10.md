## Portal Review Checklist

Before any bid decision, open the full CanadaBuys/MERX notice and check:

- Is this a public open tender, RFQ, directed contract, supply arrangement, or renewal?
- Does it require Samsung authorized reseller/partner status?
- Does it require proof of OEM authorization or manufacturer letter?
- Does it require an existing federal supply arrangement?
- Does it require bilingual support, security clearance, insurance, bonding, or mandatory references?
- Is it asking for a product SKU/entitlement only, or actual support delivery?
- Are there mandatory certifications IIS does not hold?
- Are subcontractors or partner fulfillment allowed?
- Is pricing a fixed SKU quote or service-labor quote?
- Are there penalties, liquidated damages, or non-refundable commitments?
