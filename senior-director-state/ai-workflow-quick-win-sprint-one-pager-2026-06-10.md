# AI Workflow Quick-Win Sprint

Updated: 2026-06-10T22:50:00-04:00

## What it is
- A focused AI workflow sprint for teams that know repetitive work is wasting time but do not want a vague strategy deck or a huge automation program.
- Built as a short implementation-first entry point: pick one painful workflow, prototype the better path, and leave the buyer with SOP and adoption guidance.

## Best buyers
- Operations leaders with repeated manual handoffs
- Project managers or PMO leads buried in status chasing and document follow-up
- Finance or admin teams repeating the same file, email, and reporting work
- Owners who know one recurring process is draining capacity every week
- Internal IT or support-adjacent teams that want one practical AI workflow before broader rollout

## Problems it solves
- Repeated customer or internal questions answered manually
- Slow intake, follow-up, summary, or status-reporting work
- Scattered documents and handoffs with no clean operating path
- Teams curious about AI but stuck between hype and no execution

## IIS outcome
- One selected workflow mapped clearly enough to improve
- A practical AI workflow or internal assistant prototype
- SOP and adoption notes so the team can keep using the new flow
- Clear call on what IIS can extend next versus what should stay manual

## Safe scope language
- intake and follow-up workflow cleanup
