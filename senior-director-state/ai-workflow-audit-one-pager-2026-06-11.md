## What it is
- A short discovery-first offer for teams that know repetitive work is slowing them down but are not ready to jump straight into implementation.
- Built as the lowest-friction ARIA entry point: inspect one business workflow, rank the waste/risk, and leave the buyer with a practical next-step map.

## Best buyers
- Operations leaders dealing with repeated handoffs and unclear ownership
- Finance, admin, clinic, legal, and project teams with recurring intake and follow-up work
- Owners who suspect AI or automation could help but want a safe first step
- Internal IT or support-adjacent teams trying to reduce repeated questions before building something larger

## Problems it solves
- Teams know work is repetitive but cannot clearly describe where the real bottleneck sits
- Buyers are curious about AI but do not want a vague strategy deck
- Manual steps, repeated questions, and scattered files make it hard to choose the first workflow worth fixing
- Leadership wants a short review and a sane pilot recommendation before approving deeper work

## IIS outcome
- One workflow or operating pain area mapped clearly enough to act on
- Ranked opportunity list with quick wins, risks, and what should stay manual
- Practical recommendation on whether the next step should be a quick-win sprint, intake cleanup, KB work, or no action
- Executive summary that a buyer can use internally without needing a big consulting deliverable

## Safe scope language
- workflow discovery and pain ranking
- repeated intake, follow-up, document, and summary tasks
- AI-suitable versus human-only step review
- quick-win opportunity scoring and pilot recommendation
- scoped review, not full transformation planning

## Avoid claiming publicly without approval
- guaranteed time savings or ROI percentages
- deep integration work before systems are reviewed
- enterprise transformation language
- autonomous decision-making without human review
- fixed pricing beyond a short audit posture

## Website/service-page copy
AI Workflow Audit is a short discovery-first review for teams that know repetitive work is wasting time but want a sane first step before a bigger automation push. IIS maps one workflow, ranks the friction, and leaves the buyer with a practical pilot recommendation instead of vague AI theory.

## Outreach-safe wording
IIS is packaging a focused AI Workflow Audit for teams dealing with repeated manual handoffs, scattered process knowledge, or recurring follow-up work. The goal is not a giant automation program. It is a short review that identifies the best first workflow to fix, what should stay manual, and whether a quick implementation sprint is actually worth doing.
