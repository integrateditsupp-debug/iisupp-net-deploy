## Monitor Rule

Only report leads that pass the IIS fit-only filter:

- No defence/security-clearance dependency.
- No mandatory SOC 2, ISO 27001, cyber certification, or auditor attestation at proposal time unless a real partner/proof path is available.
- No mandatory municipal references unless IIS has approved references or a named partner.
- No reseller-only/CSP/OEM partner requirement unless IIS has that partner path.
- No bid bond, performance bond, surety, or heavy insurance surprise.
- No large onsite staffing footprint that IIS cannot currently provide.
- No state/provincial/local licensing requirement IIS cannot satisfy.

Best-fit categories:

- AI readiness, AI automation, responsible AI, Copilot/M365 readiness.
- Microsoft 365, Azure, Entra, Intune, SharePoint, Teams, support workflows.
- ITSM, service desk, L1-L3, ServiceNow-style process, documentation, training.
- Data/analytics governance, reporting, knowledge management, workflow automation.
- Digital strategy, IT project management, EDRMS/SharePoint integration advisory.
