# Sourcewell RFP 061726 - Pricing Strategy

Created: 2026-06-05
Status: draft for Ahmad review; not final pricing

## Pricing Objective

Pricing is now the largest single score area after Amendment 1: 250 out of 1000 points. The proposal should be easy to evaluate, hard to misunderstand, and commercially attractive for public agencies.

Recommended structure:

- Fixed-fee packages for easy entry purchases.
- Not-to-exceed hourly rates for advisory, implementation, development, training, and managed services.
- Monthly managed AI operations tiers for recurring revenue.
- Volume discounts for larger participating entities.
- Transparent assumptions and exclusions.
- Administrative fee included in the offered prices so agencies are not separately charged.

## Recommended Position

IIS should not look like "one consultant with an hourly rate." It should look like a public-sector AI services catalog that can be bought repeatedly.

Recommended public pricing language:

"All prices are not-to-exceed Sourcewell Master Agreement prices. IIS may quote lower prices to Participating Entities based on scope, volume, timeline, and approved discounts. Prices include the proposed Sourcewell administrative fee and exclude applicable taxes, third-party software subscriptions, cloud consumption, travel, and pass-through costs unless specifically included in a statement of work."

## What Costs Are Not Included

Clearly exclude these unless specifically included in an SOW:

- Third-party AI/model/API/platform licensing.
- Cloud compute, storage, networking, or managed service provider charges.
- Customer environment licensing such as Microsoft, ServiceNow, CRM, analytics, or security tooling.
- Travel and onsite expenses.
- Independent penetration testing or external audit fees.
- Hardware.
- Taxes, duties, withholding, or government-imposed fees.
- Translation/localization beyond agreed English deliverables.

## Pricing Narrative Points

How costs scale:

- Advisory and governance work scales by number of business units, workflows, stakeholder groups, and policy complexity.
- Implementation work scales by number of systems integrated, volume/complexity of data, number of user groups, testing needs, and security/privacy controls.
- Managed operations scale by number of AI workflows/models, users supported, reporting frequency, and support coverage.
- Training scales by number of sessions, participant groups, and role-specific materials.

What triggers price changes:

- Approved SOW expansion.
- Additional systems, workflows, environments, or jurisdictions.
- Third-party licensing or cloud consumption changes.
- Major model/platform changes requested by the Participating Entity.
- Travel/onsite requirements.
- Annual published rate-card updates allowed under the Master Agreement process.

How agencies estimate total cost:

- Select a fixed package for entry or discovery work.
- Add any role-based implementation hours using not-to-exceed rates.
- Add recurring managed AI operations tier if post-launch support is required.
- Add known third-party subscriptions or cloud consumption separately.
- Apply approved volume discounts.
