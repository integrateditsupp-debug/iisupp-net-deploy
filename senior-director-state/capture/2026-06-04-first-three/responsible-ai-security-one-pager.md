## Operating Principle

Integrated IT Support Inc. uses a human-accountability-first model for AI enablement. AI is used to assist discovery, documentation, triage, workflow improvement, and user guidance. Final decisions, external commitments, sensitive data handling, and operational accountability remain with authorized humans.

## Responsible AI Controls

- Define the business purpose and success measure before implementation.
- Classify use cases by risk, data sensitivity, user impact, and automation level.
- Keep humans in the loop for decisions, escalations, and external actions.
- Document prompts, workflows, assumptions, limitations, and approval gates.
- Test outputs before production use.
- Monitor quality, adoption, errors, and user feedback.
- Maintain escalation paths for incorrect, unsafe, or unclear AI output.
- Review model/tool/vendor changes before operational use.

## Data Handling Principles

- Use only approved data sources and approved tools.
- Minimize data shared with AI systems.
- Do not use client data for model training unless explicitly authorized in writing and compliant with contract terms.
- Avoid entering sensitive, regulated, classified, or protected information into AI systems unless the environment is approved for that data.
- Maintain access control, role-based permissions, and need-to-know handling.
- Use approved retention, deletion, and audit practices.

## Security And Privacy Practices

- Align delivery with client security requirements and contract terms.
- Support privacy-by-design and least-privilege access.
- Use documented intake, change, approval, and incident processes.
- Maintain audit-ready records of decisions, changes, and approvals where required.
- Apply extra restrictions for public-sector, healthcare, financial, legal, and defence environments.

## Public-Sector Fit

IIS experience includes regulated financial services, healthcare support, municipal/public-sector support, professional services, and enterprise IT operations. This background informs an AI delivery model that prioritizes reliability, privacy, documentation, escalation, and measurable service improvement over uncontrolled automation.

## What IIS Will Not Do

- IIS will not claim AI autonomy where human accountability is required.
- IIS will not process sensitive buyer data in unapproved AI tools.
- IIS will not replace buyer security/privacy review.
- IIS will not overstate ARIA maturity or certifications.
- IIS will not submit bids, send external communications, or make legal/pricing commitments without Ahmad approval.
