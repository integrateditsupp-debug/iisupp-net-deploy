# Integrated IT Support Inc. - Resource Bench And Surge Capacity Statement

Status: draft for Ahmad review; do not submit or send without approval
Use for: MERX / CanadaBuys / Sourcewell / partner discussions

## Purpose

This statement supports Integrated IT Support Inc.'s public-sector bid responses by describing how IIS will provide sufficient delivery capacity while keeping senior accountability, quality control, and compliance discipline in place.

## Resource Model

Integrated IT Support Inc. uses a senior-led delivery model supported by a flexible specialist bench. Ahmad Wasee remains the accountable senior lead for scope control, delivery quality, escalation, client communication, and technical governance. When a project requires additional capacity or specialized skills, IIS can assign qualified subcontractors or partner resources under IIS direction, subject to the client's procurement, security, confidentiality, and approval requirements.

This model is designed for public-sector buyers that need reliable delivery without unnecessary overhead. IIS keeps the delivery structure clear: one accountable lead, defined roles, documented responsibilities, controlled onboarding, and transparent escalation.

## Standing Capacity Areas

IIS can scale delivery through standby support in the following areas:

- L1/L2/L3 IT support and helpdesk overflow.
- Microsoft 365, Azure, Entra ID, Intune, endpoint, and Active Directory support.
- ServiceNow / ITSM workflow, ticketing, SLA/KPI reporting, escalation, and documentation.
- AI readiness, AI adoption planning, responsible AI implementation support, and workflow discovery.
- AI implementation engineering for approved automation, integration, and pilot work.
- Technical writing, SOPs, runbooks, knowledge-base articles, onboarding guides, and support documentation.
- Courseware support, training materials, formatting, version control, QA, and maintenance support where qualified resources are assigned.
- Privacy, security, data handling, and responsible AI advisory support where specialized review is required.
- Project coordination, status reporting, risk tracking, issue management, and stakeholder communication.

## Delivery Controls

All assigned resources, whether internal, subcontracted, or partner-provided, will work under defined delivery controls:

- Role descriptions and responsibility matrix.
- Resume and qualification review before assignment.
- Client approval where required by the RFP or contract.
- Security, confidentiality, and data-handling requirements before access.
- Documented onboarding.
- Task register, issue log, risk log, and decision log.
- QA checklist for deliverables.
- Weekly or contract-defined status reporting.
- Escalation to Ahmad Wasee as senior accountable lead.

## Quality And Accountability

IIS does not treat subcontracting as a handoff. Specialist support is used to strengthen delivery capacity while IIS retains accountability for coordination, quality, communication, and delivery management unless the contract requires a different structure.

For procurement responses, IIS will only name specific subcontractors, partners, or resources after confirming availability, qualifications, security requirements, client approval rules, and Ahmad's approval.

## Procurement-Safe Language

Recommended language for proposals:

> Integrated IT Support Inc. is structured as a senior-led delivery organization with access to standby specialist capacity for surge support and role-specific expertise. IIS will assign qualified resources based on the final statement of work, security requirements, location requirements, and client approval process. Ahmad Wasee will remain the senior accountable lead for delivery governance, quality, escalation, and client communication.

Recommended language for DND / CFSMI:

> Where the solicitation requires specific courseware, security, or resource qualifications, IIS will provide only resources that meet the stated mandatory criteria and will identify partner or subcontractor support where required. IIS will not assign resources to controlled work until security, confidentiality, tool access, and client approval requirements are satisfied.

Recommended language for invited-Supply-Arrangement-holder opportunities:

> Where a requirement is competed among qualified invited Supply Arrangement holders, IIS may participate as a subcontractor or specialist support bench under an eligible prime. IIS will respect all procurement rules regarding document access, confidentiality, communications with the Crown, security, and prime-subcontractor responsibilities.

## Statements To Avoid

Do not say:

- IIS has employees or clearances that are not confirmed.
- IIS has DND past performance unless evidence exists.
- Named subcontractors are committed unless they have agreed.
- ARIA or any AI tool will process DND or sensitive public-sector content unless explicitly authorized.
- IIS can satisfy a mandatory resource category before the official RFP documents are reviewed.
