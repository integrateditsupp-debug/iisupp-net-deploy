## Senior Director Winner Profile

This is a different animal than the AI framework bids. A winning candidate here will be judged on responsiveness, exact resource compliance, security fit, courseware support experience, availability, rates, and low-risk execution for a defence training environment.

The buyer likely wants a reliable team that can support courseware development and maintenance without disrupting training operations. The winning submission should feel disciplined, quiet, and defence-ready:

- Named qualified resources or a credible bench.
- Clear courseware development lifecycle.
- Maintenance and change-control process.
- Documentation discipline.
- Familiarity with secure environments and need-to-know handling.
- Experience with learning materials, LMS/support tools, technical writing, QA, multimedia, accessibility, and bilingual support if required.
- Availability by the deadline and confidence that resources can work under DND/PSPC conditions.

## Bid Position

Recommended posture: conditional direct bid if IIS can satisfy every mandatory resource/security requirement; otherwise partner/subcontract under a qualified federal supplier.

Win theme: "Low-risk courseware and IT support resources for a mission-critical defence intelligence training school."

Do not lead with ARIA. For this bid, ARIA is a supporting differentiator only: workflow automation, knowledge management, documentation, and support discipline. The core story must be resource capability and training support.

## What I Would Submit - Technical Approach

### 1. Mobilization

- Confirm resource onboarding requirements, security status, tools, reporting format, and points of contact.
- Establish work intake, task priority levels, turnaround expectations, and escalation path.
- Review existing courseware standards, templates, repositories, naming conventions, and maintenance backlog.
- Create a 30-day transition and stabilization plan.

### 2. Courseware Development And Maintenance

- Receive courseware change requests through an agreed intake process.
- Analyze change impact across lesson plans, instructor guides, student materials, assessments, multimedia, and support documents.
- Maintain version control and traceability for all updates.
- Draft and update course materials using CFSMI templates and standards.
- Conduct peer QA for technical accuracy, formatting, accessibility, broken references, and consistency.
- Package deliverables for review and approval.

### 3. Associated IT Support Functions

- Provide technical documentation and user support for courseware systems and supporting workflows.
- Troubleshoot approved learning/support tools within defined authority.
- Maintain support logs, recurring issue lists, and improvement recommendations.
- Coordinate with DND/CAF stakeholders as directed.
- Support reporting, dashboards, and status tracking where requested.

### 4. Quality Management

- Use a quality checklist for every deliverable.
- Maintain issue and decision logs.
- Track all changes to request ID, requester, course/module, version, due date, status, and acceptance.
- Provide weekly status reporting with completed work, open items, risks, blockers, and next actions.
- Escalate schedule, access, data, or security issues early.

### 5. Security And Professional Conduct

- Ensure resources comply with all security, privacy, information handling, site access, and need-to-know requirements.
- Do not remove, reproduce, or reuse DND content outside the authorized environment.
- Use only approved tools, repositories, and communications channels.
- Keep AI tools out of controlled or sensitive material unless explicitly authorized in writing.
