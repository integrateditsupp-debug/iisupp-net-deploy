- The Government of Canada public AI supplier page says qualified suppliers are grouped by band: Band 1 up to CAD 1M, Band 2 up to CAD 4M, and Band 3 up to CAD 37.5M before taxes.

## Senior Director Winner Profile

Strong candidates are not only model builders. The public list shows that Canada accepts a mix of startups, midsize specialists, public companies, software firms, consulting firms, IT service providers, defence firms, cloud vendors, training firms, and data companies. The common thread is a credible ability to deliver responsible AI outcomes with documented experience, repeatable methods, security/privacy controls, and evidence.

The submission should therefore sell IIS as:

- A practical AI implementation and managed-support firm, not a speculative research lab.
- A Canadian IT operator that understands service desk, knowledge management, workflow automation, client support, and accountable delivery.
- A responsible-AI partner that can help departments complete Algorithmic Impact Assessment inputs, define human oversight, control data exposure, document decisions, monitor outputs, and train users.
- An ARIA-backed automation company with hands-on product experience in AI-assisted support, triage, knowledge retrieval, operations command centers, and AI-enabled business workflows.

## What I Would Submit - Executive Summary

Integrated IT Support Inc. submits this response to qualify for the Government of Canada's Artificial Intelligence Source List as a practical, responsible AI implementation partner focused on AI-enabled support automation, workflow automation, knowledge management, and managed AI operations.

IIS helps organizations move from AI interest to usable AI systems that are governed, secure, measurable, and supportable. Our ARIA work demonstrates the company's hands-on capability in real-time AI assistance, operational command-center design, AI-guided knowledge retrieval, support triage, browser and workflow automation, and human-in-the-loop delivery. We do not position AI as a replacement for accountability. We position AI as an operational layer that helps trained people resolve work faster, document decisions better, reduce repetitive manual effort, and improve service quality.

Our proposed services for Government of Canada departments include AI readiness assessments, workflow discovery, responsible-AI implementation planning, prototype and pilot delivery, AI assistant configuration, knowledge-base automation, Microsoft 365 and cloud workflow integration, support desk automation, model/vendor selection support, user training, monitoring, and post-launch managed support.

IIS will align delivery with Government of Canada responsible-AI guidance, including Algorithmic Impact Assessment support where automated decision-making is in scope, privacy/security review, human oversight, accessibility, transparent documentation, and ongoing performance monitoring. Our delivery model is deliberately practical: define the business outcome, classify risk, map data flows, pilot narrowly, measure results, train users, document controls, and operate the solution with clear escalation paths.

We are requesting qualification in the band supported by our submitted project evidence and resource capacity. IIS is prepared to work as a prime supplier for appropriately sized engagements and as a specialist subcontractor or partner for larger multi-disciplinary AI programs.
