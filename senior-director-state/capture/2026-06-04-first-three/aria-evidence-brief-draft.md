## What ARIA Is

ARIA is Integrated IT Support Inc.'s internal AI-enabled support and workflow framework. It is being built and operated by Ahmad Wasee to support practical AI workflows including procurement intelligence, contract hunting, prospect enrichment, knowledge retrieval, operational briefings, automation, and support-service improvement concepts.

ARIA should be presented as a developing/internal framework unless final product maturity, security, and deployment evidence is approved. The strongest procurement use is as proof that IIS has hands-on experience designing AI-assisted workflows, multi-agent coordination, MCP-style tool orchestration, knowledge structures, and human-in-the-loop operations.

## What ARIA Demonstrates

- Multi-agent workflow orchestration.
- Scheduled autonomous task execution.
- Knowledge retrieval and file-based memory patterns.
- Procurement intelligence and contract opportunity monitoring.
- CRM/prospect enrichment workflows.
- Draft-first content generation with human review.
- Tool orchestration across browser, CRM, deployment, email draft, and scheduling systems.
- Human approval gates for external actions.
- Operational briefing and task queue creation.

## Guardrails

- ARIA is not to be described as a finished government-grade platform unless final evidence supports that claim.
- ARIA should not process sensitive public-sector or defence content unless contractually authorized and technically controlled.
- ARIA-related claims must stay tied to workflow design, support automation, knowledge assistance, and practical AI enablement.
- All external outputs, bids, outreach, pricing, legal commitments, and submissions require human approval.
