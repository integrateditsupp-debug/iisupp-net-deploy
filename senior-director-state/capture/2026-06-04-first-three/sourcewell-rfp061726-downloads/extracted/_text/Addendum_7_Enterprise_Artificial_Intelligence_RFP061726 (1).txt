Question 1: 
Can Sourcewell clarify whether the responsiveness criteria and minimum qualification 
requirements for RFP #061726 may be satisfied solely through the demonstrated AI 
experience, past performance, and references of identified subcontractors or teaming 
partners within the proposed delivery team? 
Answer 1: 
Within the Questionnaire, each proposer is required to answer the following question, 
regardless of the Lot selected: “Describe your organization’s specific role and confirm 
that your organization held primary accountability for the overall solution. If 
subcontractors were used, describe their roles and explain how 
accountability was structured and governed.” Proposers should have held primary 
responsibility for the Demonstrated Experience examples they provide. 
Question 2: 
Can you please publish the pre-bid meeting attendee list with contact information? This 
will help facilitate networking and MBE outreach.

Sourcewell will not disclose the list of pre-proposal conference attendees at this time.
