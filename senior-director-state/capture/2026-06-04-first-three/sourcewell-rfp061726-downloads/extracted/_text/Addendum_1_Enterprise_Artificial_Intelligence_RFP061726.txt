Solicitation Name: Enterprise Artificial Intelligence: Platforms, Services, and Integrated

Consider the following Question(s) and Answer(s) to be part of the above-titled 
solicitation documents. The remainder of the documents remain unchanged. 
 
Question 1: 
What forms of evidence (e.g., past performance references, certifications, compliance 
attestations) are preferred to demonstrate experience? 
 
Answer 1: 
It is left to the discretion of each proposer to determine the information or 
documentation necessary to best demonstrate their ability to serve 
Sourcewell Participating Entities and satisfy all the requirements included in the 
questionnaire tables. 
 
Question 2: 
Can respondents provide Commercial references rather than public sector references? 
 
Answer 2: 
For the purposes of this RFP, Proposers may provide references who operate in “highly 
regulated industries” as defined in RFP Section IV.A. Responsiveness Criteria. This may 
potentially include commercial or public sector references.
