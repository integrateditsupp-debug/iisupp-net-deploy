Question 1: 
If a proposer selects Lot 3 as its Primary Lot and is awarded a master agreement, will 
Participating Entities be able to procure AI technology products (consistent with Lot 1 
scope) or AI professional services (consistent with Lot 2 scope) individually from the Lot 
3 awardee—without requiring the full integrated delivery offering—or is the Lot 3 
master agreement restricted solely to engagements where both technology and services 
are delivered together as a unified solution? Additionally, does the Lot 3 award preclude 
the proposer from bidding or being considered for Lot 1 or Lot 2, or are there 
circumstances under which a Lot 3 awardee may also be eligible for separate contracts 
under Lot 1 or Lot 2? 
Answer 1: 
Per RFP Section II. B., " Proposers must select ONE (1) Primary Lot under which to 
submit a response . . . Proposers will not be penalized for complementary offerings or 
capabilities that may also align with other Lots; the Lot structure serves only as an 
organizational and evaluation mechanism and does not limit the scope of a proposer’s 
broader capabilities."

Question 11: 
What is the place of performance for this work? 
Answer 11: 
Sourcewell is requesting proposals to meet the various needs of its 50,000+ 
Participating Entities as a whole, across the United States and Canada, NOT individual 
entities’ needs. The use of Sourcewell contracts is voluntary for Participating Entities. In 
this competitive solicitation process, Sourcewell will not advise a proposer on the way it 
should submit a proposal. It is left to the discretion of each proposer to articulate and 
propose the approach that aligns with its business methods and satisfies the 
requirements of the RFP. Participating Entities’ unique needs, and Solutions from 
awarded supplier(s), will be determined if and when Participating Entities choose to 
utilize a contract resulting from this solicitation.

Question 15: 
For a proposer responding under Lot 2: AI Professional Services, can the proposer also 
include and offer complementary AI-related technology platforms, software 
subscriptions, cloud services, intelligent automation platforms, and integrated delivery 
capabilities as part of broader implementation or managed service engagements?  
 
More specifically, if the proposer’s primary value proposition and principal line of 
business align with Lot 2 professional services, would Sourcewell still permit the 
proposer to:  
Resell or provide access to third-party AI or cloud platforms as part of a services 
engagement;  
Deliver integrated implementation and managed services associated with those 
platforms;  
Include platform licensing/subscription pricing where directly tied to the proposed 
professional services engagement; and  
Market broader end-to-end AI transformation capabilities under the resulting 
agreement, provided the proposer is not positioning itself primarily as a standalone AI 
platform provider under Lot 1 or a turnkey integrated AI product provider under Lot 3?  
 
We are seeking clarification regarding how broadly complementary technology, 
software, and integrated delivery capabilities may be included within a Lot 2 response 
and resulting Master Agreement. 
Answer 15: 
Refer to RFP Section II. B. Primary Deliverable Determination and Lot 1: AI Products and  
Platforms.  
 
Sourcewell will be providing no further clarifications regarding complementary technology or 
Lot inclusions at this time. In the competitive process, Sourcewell will not advise a proposer on 
the content of the proposal. It is left to the discretion of each proposer to determine how to 
best satisfy all requirements of the questionnaire tables. 
Question 16: 
Is the Certificate of Insurance required with the response or once the vendor is 
awarded?

Answer 16: 
An awarded supplier will be expected to carry the insurance coverage as stated in the 
Master Agreement, Article 2, 22. upon execution. To identify any exception, or to 
request any modification, to Sourcewell’s standard terms, conditions, or specifications, 
a proposer must submit the proposed exception(s) or requested modification(s) via 
redline in the Master Agreement provided in the “Documents” section of the “Bid 
Details” page on the Sourcewell Procurement Portal and uploaded as part of its 
response. Only those exceptions noted at the time of the proposal submission will be 
considered.
