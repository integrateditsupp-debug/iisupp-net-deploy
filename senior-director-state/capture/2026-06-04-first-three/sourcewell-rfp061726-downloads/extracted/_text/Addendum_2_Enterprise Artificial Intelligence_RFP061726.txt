Question 1: 
I can't seem to find the actual RFP questionnaire anywhere in these bid documents, am I 
simply missing something obvious or has this not been posted yet? 
Answer 1: 
The Questionnaire is embedded within the portal and becomes accessible after selecting 
“Start Submission” on the Bid Details page. For additional guidance on responding to the 
RFP, including completing the Questionnaire, please refer to the Submit Response Guide 
available on the Procurement Portal main page. 
 
Question 2: 
Please clarify the relationship between this RFP and RFP 110724 (Artificial 
Intelligence Readiness, Implementation, and Support Services) awarded in April 
2025? Specifically, should respondents view this as a continuation, expansion, or 
separate procurement, and how will any overlap in scope or services be 
managed? 
 

--- PAGE 2 ---
Answer 2: 
This RFP is a separate procurement from RFP 110724 awarded in April 2025 and should 
not be viewed as a continuation or extension. AI needs have expanded significantly 
since that award, and this solicitation reflects a broader, more advanced scope of 
solutions. 
 
Participation in or award under RFP 110724 does not guarantee or influence selection 
for this RFP. While there may be a partial overlap in scope, all responses will be 
evaluated independently based on the requirements of this solicitation.
