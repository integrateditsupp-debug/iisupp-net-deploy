Question 7: 
If a potential bidder previously provided professional services to Sourcewell that 
contributed to the development of this solicitation, does that create an organizational 
conflict of interest that would disqualify the vendor from responding to RFP 061726. If 
so, would there be a disclosure or mitigation process available. 
Answer 7: 
Per the Code of Federal Regulations, “To ensure objective contractor performance, and 
eliminate unfair competitive advantage, contractors that develop or draft specifications, 
requirements, statements of work, or invitations for bids must be excluded from 
competing on those procurements” (2 C.F.R. § 200.319(b)).

Question 9:   
Can the Master Agreement be awarded to a Canadian prime (Supplier) that 
subcontracts defined delivery activities to a South African subcontractor? 
Are there any restrictions on the subcontractor’s location, or any specific 
requirements we should address in our proposal regarding subcontractor 
oversight, compliance, or reporting? 
 
If the above model is acceptable, please confirm what level of subcontractor 
detail should be provided at proposal stage (e.g., named subcontractor(s), 
roles/responsibilities, percentages of work, and any supporting documentation). 
Answer 9: 
Refer to RFP Section II. D. Single Primary Lot, “…  If proposer includes the equipment, 
products, and services of its subsidiary entities, the proposer must also identify all 
subsidiaries included in its proposal. If proposer requires the use of distributors, dealers, 
resellers, or subcontractors to provide the equipment, products, or services, the 
proposal must address how the equipment, products or services will be provided to 
Participating Entities, and describe the network of distributors, dealers, resellers, and/or 
subcontractors that will be available to serve Participating Entities under a resulting 
master agreement.”  
 
It is left to the discretion of each proposer to determine the information or 
documentation necessary to best demonstrate their ability to serve Sourcewell 
Participating Entities and satisfy all the requirements of the RFP and the questionnaire 
tables. Proposals are evaluated based on the criteria stated in the RFP.
