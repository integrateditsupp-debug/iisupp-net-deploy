## Executive Readiness View

This is a 10-year cooperative master agreement, not a single project. Sourcewell will evaluate whether IIS can become a public-sector-ready AI services supplier for participating entities across the United States and Canada.

Current recommended bid posture:

- Submit under Lot 2 - AI Professional Services.
- Present IIS as the accountable prime for AI advisory, governance, workflow automation, implementation, training, and managed AI operations.
- Present ARIA as applied internal evidence and reusable delivery know-how, not as the primary commercial platform unless Ahmad approves stronger production/customer evidence.
- Include a clear subcontractor/resource bench model, but do not rely on subcontractor past performance alone for responsiveness.
- Build pricing as fixed packages plus not-to-exceed role rates, with volume discounting and a plain-language total-cost narrative.

## Responsiveness Criteria

Sourcewell requires proposers to demonstrate experience delivering AI solutions in a highly regulated industry at a scale appropriate to the selected Lot. The exact thresholds are in the portal questionnaire under Responsiveness Criteria.

Qualifying regulated industries include banking and financial services, insurance, healthcare and public health, federal/state/local/tribal government, K-12 or higher education, public safety and criminal justice, public transportation, regulated utilities/energy, and defense/national security.

IIS evidence alignment:

| Requirement | Best IIS Evidence | Strength | Gap / Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Highly regulated industry | Raymond James, RBC Capital Markets, Scotia, Ontario Health, City of Toronto, IBM | Strong | Need exact project references and permission-safe descriptions |
| AI solution delivery | ARIA, Raymond James AI/automation, M365/Copilot/Azure OpenAI, RAG/multi-agent workflows | Moderate to strong | Need concrete examples with dates, outcomes, and Ahmad's role |
| Scale appropriate to Lot 2 | Enterprise IT operations, regulated environments, migration/support programs | Moderate | Need describe scale truthfully without overclaiming contract ownership |
| Primary accountability | Founder/IIS and senior delivery roles | Moderate | Addenda say proposer should have held primary responsibility for demonstrated examples; do not rely only on subcontractors |
| Public-sector fit | Ontario Health, City of Toronto, healthcare/public workflows | Moderate | Need any public-sector AI-specific evidence if available |
