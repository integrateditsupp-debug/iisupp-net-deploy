## Best Bid Strategy

### Sourcewell

Go Lot 2 unless Ahmad can prove ARIA is a mature commercial platform-plus-services offering. Lot 2 lets us win on professional services: AI readiness, governance, pilots, automation, managed operations, training, and implementation support.

Why this can score: Sourcewell cares about pricing, depth/breadth, delivery ability, marketing plan, value-add, and viability. Our proposal must include packages and not-to-exceed pricing, not just "call us for hourly work."

### PSPC AI Source List

Qualify at the highest band supported by real past performance. The public supplier list proves small/startup suppliers can qualify, but the band is evidence-driven. We should not overreach. A clean Band 1 or Band 2 qualification is more valuable than a rejected Band 3 attempt.

Why this can score: IIS/ARIA is a credible fit for practical AI support automation, workflow automation, knowledge-base automation, and responsible AI implementation.

### DND CFSMI

Treat this as a disciplined resource proposal, not an AI pitch. The winning path is named resources, clearance, courseware support, documentation, QA, change control, and reliable delivery.

Why this can score: IIS already has support-operations instincts. The missing piece is proving exact resource categories and security compliance.
