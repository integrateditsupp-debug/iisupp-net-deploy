## Senior Director Winner Profile

Successful Sourcewell-style AI candidates look like companies that can be bought from by many public agencies without extra handholding. They show:

- A clear catalog of services with not-to-exceed pricing and discounts.
- Public-sector readiness: procurement-friendly terms, onboarding, references, support coverage, and reporting.
- Strong AI governance and implementation methods, not just AI buzzwords.
- Enough delivery capacity to support multiple participating entities.
- A marketing plan that helps Sourcewell drive usage after award.
- Specific examples: Infojini's awarded contract emphasizes AI roadmapping, data readiness/governance, chatbots/agentic AI, model development, automation/RPA, compliant solutions, staff augmentation, cloud, Salesforce/Microsoft, and AI cybersecurity. Cadence emphasizes AI readiness/Copilot assessments, M365, SharePoint/Purview, pilots, custom AI, training, integration, and ongoing support.

## Bid Position

Recommended lot: Lot 2 - AI Professional Services.

Reason: IIS/ARIA has a credible professional-services story around AI readiness, workflow automation, support automation, responsible AI policy, governance-aware implementation, managed AI operations, M365/cloud workflow integration, and custom implementation. Lot 3 is attractive but riskier because the downloaded scope document says Lot 3 is for a commercially established unified technology-and-services offering with end-to-end accountability. Unless Ahmad can prove ARIA is already a mature, commercially sold platform with support/service delivery under one offering, Lot 2 is the cleaner winning path.

Win theme: "Practical AI adoption for public agencies: readiness, governance, pilots, workflow automation, support automation, and managed operations from an IT operator that knows how to support real users."

## What I Would Submit - Executive Summary

Integrated IT Support Inc. proposes a practical AI professional-services program for Sourcewell participating entities. The program is designed for public agencies that need the benefits of AI but cannot accept uncontrolled data exposure, unclear accountability, weak user adoption, or unsupported pilots.

IIS will provide six service lines:

1. AI readiness and maturity assessment.
2. Responsible AI governance and policy implementation.
3. AI workflow automation and support automation.
4. Pilot, proof-of-concept, and custom AI implementation.
5. AI-enabled managed services and operations.
6. Training, adoption, and ongoing improvement.

Our approach is designed for repeatable public-sector delivery. Every engagement begins with an outcome map, risk classification, data-flow review, stakeholder interview, and value case. We then define the smallest viable pilot, document human oversight and escalation, establish success metrics, implement the automation, train users, and transition to managed support. This avoids expensive AI theater and creates measurable operational wins.

Typical agency outcomes include:

- Faster service desk and citizen-service triage.
- Better internal knowledge retrieval.
- Reduced repetitive manual routing and drafting.
- Cleaner documentation and reporting.
- Improved staff AI literacy and policy compliance.
- Safer use of AI tools in Microsoft 365, cloud, CRM, ticketing, and line-of-business workflows.
- Ongoing monitoring, improvement, and support after launch.

IIS is especially strong for agencies that want AI to improve operational workflows, IT support, customer service, back-office process handling, knowledge management, and staff productivity. We are not a reseller pushing a single model. We are a delivery partner that helps agencies select the right tool, define controls, integrate responsibly, and operate the result.
