## 6. Executive Summary Drafts

### PSPC AI Source List Executive Summary

Integrated IT Support Inc. seeks qualification for the PSPC Artificial Intelligence Source List as a senior-led technology support and AI enablement company. IIS focuses on practical AI adoption in operational environments: workflow discovery, support automation, knowledge-base assistance, AI readiness, governance-aware implementation, and human-in-the-loop service improvement.

IIS is building ARIA as an internal AI-enabled support and workflow framework. ARIA should be presented carefully as a developing platform and operational capability, not as a finished government-grade product unless and until that evidence is available. The relevance to Canada is practical: ARIA-informed methods help IIS design safe support triage concepts, knowledge assistance, user guidance, and responsible AI adoption plans.

IIS will align work with public-sector expectations for privacy, security, accessibility, documentation, human oversight, and measurable service improvement. Qualification should be requested only at the band supported by documented IIS project evidence.

### Enterprise AI Executive Summary

Integrated IT Support Inc. proposes to support Sourcewell participating entities under Lot 2: AI Professional Services. IIS provides senior-led technology support and AI enablement for organizations that need practical delivery, clear governance, responsible adoption, and reliable user support.

IIS will help public-sector organizations assess AI readiness, identify safe and valuable use cases, document governance, pilot narrowly, train staff, support users, and measure service improvement. Our approach is deliberately disciplined: human accountability first, AI assistance second, and measurable service improvement throughout.

IIS is best suited to agencies seeking AI readiness, workflow discovery, internal support automation, knowledge-base assistance, ticket triage concepts, user guidance, and managed improvement around existing IT and business operations.

### DND CFSMI Executive Summary

Integrated IT Support Inc. proposes a disciplined support model for CFSMI courseware development, maintenance, and associated IT support functions, subject to confirmation of all mandatory resource and security requirements.

IIS understands that CFSMI supports mission-critical defence intelligence training. The proposed approach prioritizes reliable support, clean documentation, controlled change management, clear escalation, user support, quality assurance, and respect for defence/public-sector process.

For this opportunity, IIS should not lead with AI. ARIA and AI-assisted workflows may be referenced only as internal methods for improving support process design, knowledge management, and documentation, and only where permitted by the contract and security requirements.
