- Data infrastructure.

Proposers may include complementary products and services that support deployment, operation, or scaling of AI solutions, even when those offerings are not exclusively AI-specific, if they align with the solicitation scope.

## Lot Structure

The RFP is organized into three response Lots. Proposers must select one primary Lot.

### Lot 1 - AI Products And Platforms

Includes AI platforms, infrastructure, model development/deployment/lifecycle, enterprise knowledge management/data infrastructure, agentic AI platforms, workflow automation platforms, document intelligence, multimodal processing, and unstructured data extraction at enterprise scale.

Lot 1 requires AI to be a primary standalone component of the offering.

IIS fit: moderate only if ARIA is positioned as a developing/internal framework; not the recommended primary Lot unless ARIA maturity evidence is much stronger.

### Lot 2 - AI Professional Services

Includes:

- Enterprise AI maturity assessment.
- Responsible AI policy development.
- AI governance architecture.
- Operating model redesign.
- AI investment prioritization and business case development.
- Workforce transformation strategy.
- Cross-sector AI policy advisory.
- AI operations management.
- Model monitoring.
- Model drift management.
- Continuous improvement programs.
- Managed AI services for government functions.
- AI security operations.
- Custom AI-enabled applications, integrations, and automated workflows.
- Model fine-tuning/adaptation.
- Client-specific solution design.

IIS fit: strongest primary Lot. Ahmad's resume and IIS/ARIA positioning align directly with AI readiness, responsible AI, workflow discovery, support automation, M365/Azure/Copilot support, managed AI operations, training, documentation, and implementation support.

### Lot 3 - Integrated AI Delivery

Lot 3 is for a single organization capable of delivering both AI technology products and AI professional services under one contract, serving as the primary accountable party for design, integration, performance, and delivery of integrated AI outcomes.

The document is clear that simply having technology and services is not enough. The integrated technology-plus-services model must be a standard, unified, commercially established offering.

IIS fit: not recommended as primary Lot at this time unless Ahmad approves stronger evidence that ARIA is commercially established as a unified platform-plus-services offering.

## Recommended Lot Decision

Recommended primary Lot: Lot 2 - AI Professional Services.

Reason:

- Best match to Ahmad's resume and IIS's actual delivery capacity.
- Avoids overclaiming ARIA as a mature standalone platform.
- Lets IIS include complementary support services such as data infrastructure, cybersecurity-aware delivery, compliance, M365/Azure workflows, documentation, training, managed support, and custom workflow automation.
- Keeps the bid credible while still ambitious.
