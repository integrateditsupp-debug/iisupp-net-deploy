## Rules for future expansion

1. Prefer official public procurement, supplier, or code-of-conduct pages.
2. Prefer low-cost entry modes in this order:
   - public intake email with official field list
   - public self-registration portal
   - public procurement page with contact path
   - invitation-led or relationship-first watchlist
3. Do not claim supplier approval, diversity status, certifications, or enterprise experience that is not verified.
4. Stop before Send, Register, Create Account, Submit, Certify, Sign, or risky Publish.
