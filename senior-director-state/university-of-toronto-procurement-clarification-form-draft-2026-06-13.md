- The Prospective Suppliers page says suppliers should respond through MERX/Biddingo for open opportunities, or contact relevant faculties/departments directly for purchases below CAD 121,200.

- The public inquiry form is the cleanest visible route to ask a factual process question without pretending IIS already has a sponsoring department or approved-supplier status.
