- The command system already identified this gap: the site needed a clearer path from ARIA, Services, Shop, and Growth Library into one deliberate first action.
- This slice reduces CTA fragmentation and makes IIS easier to understand for buyers who are interested but not yet sure whether they need a service conversation, a workflow audit, or a proof-first pack.
- It also fixes a quieter monetization gap on the local-only page: some buyers could see the first CTA, but not the safer second-step path after a first win.
- It strengthens the product-to-service ladder without forcing every visitor into the same funnel:
  - scoping for urgent service work

  - pack-first for cautious or self-serve buyers
- The new follow-on ladders make recurring support feel earned instead of premature, which is safer for trust and better for qualified recurring revenue.
