See ARIA Sentinel work

Simulation — scripted on this page. Nothing runs on your computer.

Three everyday problems, played the way ARIA Sentinel handles them with Autonomous mode and live fixes switched on. Out of the box it starts in Manual mode with live fixes off, so it only previews. Every notice below uses the app's own wording.

ManualPreviews only

ARIA shows what a fix would do and changes nothing on the PC.

ConfirmedAsks once

Nothing runs until you say yes — except a green fix that needs no administrator rights and is vendor-vetted or already proven on this device: that one runs after the 10-second countdown you can cancel.

AutonomousThe 3-notice rule

In Autonomous mode, when ARIA finds a problem it can fix, it tells you first — 3 times, 3 minutes apart. If nobody answers, it fixes the problem on its own after a 10-second countdown you can cancel, through the same safety checks as every fix. Problems that need administrator rights or a person are never fixed on their own: after 3 unanswered notices ARIA tells your IT contact instead. Every notice lets you choose Fix now, Not now (waits 4 hours) or Never for this.

Confirmed and Autonomous come with Pro and every business tier; Personal is Manual only. The 3 minutes and the 4 hours are defaults you can change in Settings → Autonomy.

Wi-Fi connected, no internet / stale DNS

Scripted with Autonomous mode and live fixes on. The DNS refresh is a vendor-vetted green fix: it ships with a check that must pass afterwards and has nothing to undo, so ARIA may run it on its own — but only after telling you three times.
