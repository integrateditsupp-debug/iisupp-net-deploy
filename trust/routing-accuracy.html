How it's measured

Every push to main runs tests/run-all.mjs (including classifier-accuracy.test.mjs, which RECOMPUTES this number — it is never a stored string) + the 332K mega-scenario corpus (tests/run-mega-scenarios.js). Latest results: ARIA Sentinel/qa/classifier-accuracy-2026-06-25.md. Source: aria-kb-retrieval.mjs.

Why this matters

Zero of the 22 competitors we researched publish a corpus-stress-test score. The closest claims are marketing percentages with no denominator: Intercom "67% avg," IrisAgent "95% on 50% resolved," Espressive "85%." We give you the corpus, the score, the commit hash, and the cadence.
