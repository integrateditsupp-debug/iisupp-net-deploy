Response Guideline 1
Resource’s Experience 
The Resource must have experience working in a similar environment delivering work scope and complexity similar to that of the Services described in this MUL RFQ.  Following are the minimum experience requirements for each of the Service Areas represented in this MUL RFQ.  Additional years of experience beyond the minimum will result in additional points being awarded.
OnBase Administrator
The Corporation’s preference is for Resources with a minimum of five (5) years of demonstrated experience providing OnBase Administration, configuration, and engineering services for OnBase EP5 Global Cloud Services solutions.
Corporate Technology Trainer
The Corporation’s preference is for Resources with a minimum of five (5) years demonstrated experience delivering IT adult training and documentation with some of this experience gained producing deliverables using Microsoft Office and Visio tools.  Preference will be given to individuals with experience providing these services to the BC Government’s Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure (MOTI), and additional years of experience beyond the minimum providing services to MOTI will result in additional points being awarded.
Microsoft 365 Developer
The Corporation’s preference is for Resources with a minimum of three (3) years demonstrated experience working as a Microsoft 365 Developer.
Senior IT Project Manager
The Corporation’s preference is for Resources with a minimum five (5) years demonstrated experience working as a project manager in an IT environment with some of this experience gained producing deliverables using Microsoft Office and Project Management tools.  Preference will be given to individuals with experience providing these services to the BC Government’s Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure (MOTI), and additional years of experience beyond the minimum providing services to MOTI will result in additional points being awarded.
Senior Business Analyst
The Corporation’s preference is for Resources with a minimum five (5) years of demonstrated experience working as a Senior Business Analyst with some of this experience gained producing deliverables using Microsoft Office, Visio, and Project Management tools.  Preference will be given to individuals with experience providing these services to the BC Government’s Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure (MOTI), and additional years of experience beyond the minimum providing services to MOTI will result in additional points being awarded.
Business Analyst
The Corporation’s preference is for Resources with a minimum three (3) years of demonstrated experience working as a Business Analyst with some of this experience gained producing deliverables using Microsoft Office and Visio tools.  Preference will be given to individuals with experience providing these services to the BC Government’s Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure (MOTI), and additional years of experience beyond the minimum providing services to MOTI will result in additional points being awarded.
Enterprise, Technical, and Solutions Architect
The Corporation prefers Resources with a minimum ten (10) years demonstrated experience working as an Enterprise Architect with some of this experience gained producing deliverables using Microsoft Office, Visio, and Project Management tools.  Preference will be given to individuals with experience providing these services to the BC Government’s Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure (MOTI), and additional years of experience beyond the minimum providing services to MOTI will result in additional points being awarded.
Technical Analyst
The Corporation’s preference is for Resources with a minimum of three (3) years’ demonstrated experience as a Desktop Support Analyst and/or IT Support Specialist.  Preference will be given to individuals with experience using JIRA by Atlassian.
Hyland Solutions Architect
The Corporation’s preference is for Resources with a minimum of three years’ demonstrated experience as a Hyland Solutions Architect.  .
