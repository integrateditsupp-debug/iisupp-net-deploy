 Section 1: Introduction & Organization 
 Overview 
 1.1 Purpose 
 Uncommon Schools, Inc. (Uncommon or USI) is issuing this Request for Proposals (RFP) for 
 cost-effective IT managed services to meet the business and operational needs of its schools, 
 regional teams, and Home Office (HO). Uncommon is conducting a competitive procurement 
 process to evaluate the market and identify the best managed services partner for the 
 organization going forward. All vendors will be evaluated equally under this process. 
 Uncommon Schools, Inc., in its capacity as the Charter Management Organization ("CMO") 
 across affiliated schools, is managing this public competitive contract opportunity on behalf of 
 and representing the respective needs of all affiliated schools, LEAs, regions, and offices as 
 listed in Appendix A, and in accordance with the applicable procurement requirements for public 
 charter schools in the states of New Jersey, New York, and Massachusetts and through the 
 federal Department of Education under the following provisions: 
 ●  Federal Procurement Code Uniform Guidance (2 CFR Part 200.317 et seq.) 
 ●  New Jersey Public School Contracts Law (6A:23A-22.5, N.J.S.A. 18A:18A-1 et seq.) 
 ●  New Jersey Administrative Code (N.J.A.C. 5:34 et seq.) 
 ●  Uniform Procurement Act under Massachusetts General Laws (Chapter 30b), only 
 where applicable to Commonwealth Charter Schools 
 ●  NY State Charter School Law (Article 56, Education Law sections 2850-2857) 
 Specific to North Star Academy Charter School of Newark and Camden Prep Renaissance 
 School in New Jersey, the Qualified Purchasing Agent (“QPA”) and/or an approved delegate for 
 each LEA has been consulted in the writing of this RFP and will participate in the evaluation of 
 qualifying offers to ensure compliance with New Jersey procurement requirements and school 
 interests. Specific to Roxbury Prep Charter School in Massachusetts, the MCPPO and/or an 
 approved delegate has been consulted in the writing of this RFP, will be present at the proposal 
 opening, and will participate in the evaluation of qualifying offers to ensure compliance with 
 Massachusetts procurement requirements and school interests. 
 Scope of services includes but is not limited to: 
 a. Enterprise Infrastructure Administration and Maintenance 
 b. Cloud Software and Device Management 
 c. Security and Compliance 
 d. End User Support 
 e. On-Site Support 
 f. Classroom Technology Support 
 g. End User Device Management

 k. Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Planning 
 l. Project Management Services 
 m. Multi-Vendor Support and Advocacy 
 1.2 About Uncommon Schools 
 Uncommon Schools is a nonprofit charter management organization that operates 63 locations 
 across 5 regions: New York City, NY; Newark, NJ; Camden, NJ; Rochester; NY; and Boston, 
 MA. Uncommon serves approximately 20,000 students and employs approximately 2,800 staff 
 across its school network and Home Office. Uncommon utilizes a dual leadership model where 
 Principals manage instructional staff and focus on coaching while Directors of Operation 
 manage operational staff and focus on the school’s daily operation. A full list of locations, staff, 
 and students is available in  Appendix A  . 
 Uncommon's mission is to start and manage outstanding urban public schools that close the 
 opportunity gap and prepare low-income students for success in college and beyond. Our 
 schools serve students in grades [PreK–12], and our technology environment must support the 
 full complexity of that mission — from classroom instruction to school operations to home office 
 functions.

 1.3 Strategic Context 
 Our strategic ambition is to lead the best and highest-performing PK-12 schools in the nation — 
 closing the college completion gap, providing a path to economic freedom, and extending our 
 impact to students nationally and around the world. As one of the largest school systems in the 
 country, achieving this requires that every operational decision support what happens in 
 classrooms. 
 Reliable, secure, and scalable technology infrastructure is a prerequisite for academic 
 excellence. This procurement reflects that priority. We are seeking a managed services partner 
 with deep technical expertise, demonstrated experience in large PK-12 environments, and the 
 operational capacity to support an organization of our scale and complexity. 
 1.4 Key Outcomes 
 The key outcomes of this procurement include: 
 ●  Cost efficiency — Demonstrated value relative to current spend, with transparent pricing 
 and measurable return on investment 
 ●  Capability alignment — A vendor whose core competencies match the scope and 
 complexity of services required 
 ●  Contractual accountability — Clearly defined service expectations, performance 
 standards, and remedies for non-performance 
 ●  Operational reliability — Consistent, school-centered technology support that minimizes 
 disruption and enables student and staff success

 Cybersecurity 
 The selected vendor must provide comprehensive cybersecurity services aligned with the NIST 
 Cybersecurity Framework (CSF). This scope includes: 
 ●  24/7/365 Managed Detection & Response (MDR): Continuous monitoring and triage of 
 all managed endpoints, servers, and network infrastructure. 
 ●  Tenant Governance (Microsoft & Google): Operational management of security policies, 
 conditional access, and other configuration for Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace as 
 needed. 
 ●  Threat Remediation: Execution of remediation actions (e.g.,investigating scope and 
 severity, account lockdowns, policy adjustments, etc). 
 ●  EDR Operations: Full management of the Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) 
 stack, with defined authority to isolate infected endpoints immediately upon threat 
 detection. 
 ●  Vulnerability & Lifecycle Management: Maintenance of a documented patching schedule 
 for all OS and third-party applications, including an "emergency" track for Zero-Day 
 exploits. 
 ●  Data Resilience: Management of an Immutable Backup solution for all servers hosted by 
 the MSP, as described in the RFP with quarterly restoration testing to ensure recovery 
 from ransomware or catastrophic data loss. 
 ●  Security Advisory (vCISO): Proactive recommendations for security posture 
 improvements that balance K-12 regulatory requirements (FERPA/COPPA/NYS Ed Law 
 2-d) with public school budget constraints. 
 ●  Compliance Standard: The vendor shall maintain SOC 2 Type II certification and provide 
 Uncommon with the most recent audit report annually.
