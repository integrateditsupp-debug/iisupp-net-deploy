AI Governance

Human-governed AI support

ARIA is designed to help with IT triage, knowledge retrieval, support routing, and guarded local remediation. It does not replace customer authority, legal approval, security ownership, or human review for high-risk actions.

Operating rules

  • Real-or-empty metrics only; no fabricated uptime, savings, or accuracy claims.
  • Customer-owned credentials and per-customer approval for integrations.
  • Content-blind audit records and private-folder exclusion.
  • Manual or confirmed gates before meaningful system changes.

Automated decisions

ARIA can classify support requests, suggest next steps, and route issues. Users can request human review, opt out where applicable, and see how automated support decisions are handled.
