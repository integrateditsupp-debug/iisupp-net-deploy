**Pilot Agreement** between **Integrated IT Support Inc.** ("Provider"), 30 Fothergill Court, Whitby, Ontario L1P 1L4, Canada — and **____________________** ("Client"), effective ____________ ("Start Date").

## 1. What the pilot covers

Provider grants Client a free, non-exclusive right to run ARIA Sentinel on up to **5** Client workstations for **14 calendar days** from the Start Date. The pilot covers diagnostics, guided troubleshooting, and supervised fixes. Every fix is previewed and applied only when a Client operator confirms it.

## 2. Fees

None. No payment details are collected for the pilot. Nothing in this pilot obligates Client to purchase anything.

## 3. Client data

Pilot metrics — issue counts, fix confirmations, deflection, effort estimates — are computed and stored **locally on Client machines** in plain JSON. No pilot data is transmitted to Provider without Client's explicit consent. Client may delete local pilot data at any time.

## 4. The pilot report

At the end of the pilot the software produces a report from Client's own audit log. Where the data is insufficient, the report shows an empty state rather than an estimate. Any use of Client's results in Provider marketing — including a case study — requires Client's separate written consent.

## 5. Responsibilities

Client remains responsible for its own systems and backups. Provider offers reasonable email and telephone support during the pilot at no charge. The software applies no change without an operator's confirmation.

## 6. Term and termination

Either party may end the pilot at any time, for any reason, on notice; email is sufficient. At the end of the term, or on termination, Client may uninstall the software. The product's free manual mode remains available regardless.

## 7. Warranty and liability

The pilot software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, for evaluation purposes. To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential damages arising from the pilot.

## 8. After the pilot

If Client wishes to continue, the parties may negotiate a paid plan under a separate agreement. Published plan pricing is available at iisupp.net. This template creates no automatic renewal, no obligation, and no charges.
