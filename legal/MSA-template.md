## 1. Services

Provider will provide Customer access to the ARIA SaaS platform (the "Services") under the plan tier selected in Schedule A. Services include:
- ARIA AI assistant access via web (iisupp.net/aria), mobile, and integrated channels (Teams, Slack, email) per plan tier.
- Curated knowledge base (143+ articles, growing).
- Per-tier query quota.
- Customer support per Schedule B (Service Level Agreement).
- Optional integrations per Schedule C.

Provider may add features, integrations, and KB content during the Term without additional charge unless explicitly designated as a premium add-on.

---

## 2. Term

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for the period set forth in Schedule A ("Initial Term"). After the Initial Term, it auto-renews for successive renewal periods equal in length to the Initial Term unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days before the end of the then-current period.

---

## 3. Fees & payment

Customer pays the fees set forth in Schedule A. Payment terms:
- Monthly plans: billed monthly in advance; payment due upon invoice.
- Annual plans: billed annually in advance; payment due within 30 days of invoice.
- Top-up packs (additional queries beyond plan quota): billed upon purchase via Stripe.

Fees are in USD unless otherwise specified. Applicable taxes are additional. Late payments incur interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum permitted by law.

Annual fees are non-refundable except as set forth in Section 11.

---

## 4. Customer responsibilities

Customer shall:
- Provide accurate registration and contact information.
- Designate authorised administrators.
- Ensure end-users comply with Provider's Acceptable Use Policy (Schedule D).
- Maintain the confidentiality of access credentials.
- Comply with applicable laws and regulations.
- Not reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Services.
- Not resell, sublicense, or white-label the Services except as authorised in Schedule A.

---

## 5. Intellectual property

5.1 **Provider's IP.** The Services, including software, knowledge base, symbolic state dictionary, ARIA brain, and all updates and improvements thereto, are and remain the exclusive property of Provider. Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable license to use the Services during the Term solely for Customer's internal business purposes.

5.2 **Customer's IP.** Customer retains all rights in Customer Data and Customer-uploaded knowledge base content ("Customer Content"). Customer grants Provider a limited license to process Customer Content solely to deliver the Services.

5.3 **Feedback.** Customer may voluntarily provide suggestions or feedback. Customer grants Provider a perpetual, royalty-free license to use such feedback for any purpose, including improvement of the Services.

5.4 **Aggregated data.** Provider may collect anonymised, aggregated usage data (no Personal Data) for benchmarking, product improvement, and industry reporting.

---

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to hold the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence, use it only for the purposes of this Agreement, and protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information (but not less than reasonable care). Confidential Information excludes information that is publicly known, independently developed, or rightfully obtained from a third party without confidentiality obligation. Obligations survive termination for 5 years.

---

## 7. Data protection

Each party shall comply with applicable data protection laws. The Data Processing Agreement (DPA) attached as Schedule E governs Provider's processing of Personal Data on Customer's behalf. The DPA is hereby incorporated by reference.

For healthcare customers, the Business Associate Agreement (BAA) attached as Schedule F applies and is incorporated by reference.

8.1 **Provider warrants** that:
- It has the right to provide the Services to Customer.

## 9. Indemnification

9.1 **Provider indemnification.** Provider shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer from third-party claims alleging that the Services infringe such third party's IP rights, provided Customer (i) promptly notifies Provider of the claim, (ii) gives Provider sole control of defence, and (iii) provides reasonable cooperation. Provider's maximum liability under this Section is the fees paid by Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

9.2 **Customer indemnification.** Customer shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Provider from third-party claims arising from (i) Customer's violation of law, (ii) Customer's misuse of the Services, or (iii) Customer Content (including IP infringement).

---

## 10. Limitation of liability

EXCEPT FOR LIABILITY ARISING FROM (i) WILFUL MISCONDUCT, (ii) GROSS NEGLIGENCE, (iii) DATA SECURITY BREACH ATTRIBUTABLE TO PROVIDER, OR (iv) INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 9, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT IS LIMITED TO THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER IN THE 12 MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM. NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS OR LOST DATA.

---

## 11. Termination

11.1 **Termination for cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement upon 30 days' written notice if the other materially breaches and fails to cure within the notice period.

11.2 **Termination for insolvency.** Either party may terminate immediately upon the other's bankruptcy, insolvency, or assignment for benefit of creditors.

11.3 **Termination for convenience.** Customer may terminate Monthly plans at any time effective at the end of the current billing month. Annual plans are not terminable for convenience but Customer may receive a pro-rata refund of unused months less any usage above 110% of pro-rated quota.

11.4 **Effects of termination.** Upon termination:
- Customer's access to the Services is revoked.
- Customer Data export is available for 30 days post-termination.
- Hard delete of Customer Data within 60 days, except where law requires retention.
- Customer remains liable for fees accrued through the termination date.

---

## 12. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for delays or failures caused by acts of God, war, terrorism, government action, internet outages, pandemic-related disruption, labor disputes, or other events beyond reasonable control, provided the affected party uses commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance.

---

## 13. General provisions

13.1 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent, except that either party may assign to a successor in a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.

13.2 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by email with confirmation of receipt or by overnight courier. Provider's notice address: integrateditsupp@iisupp.net. Customer's notice address: [CUSTOMER NOTICE EMAIL].

13.3 **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable therein. Disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Ontario, with each party consenting to the exclusive jurisdiction and venue.

13.4 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement (including Schedules A–F as applicable) constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. It supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and representations.

13.5 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

13.6 **Waiver.** No waiver is effective unless in writing.

13.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and via electronic signature.

13.8 **Independent contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, agency, or employment relationship.

13.9 **Publicity.** Provider may identify Customer as a customer on its website and in promotional materials, using only Customer's name and logo, unless Customer opts out in writing.
