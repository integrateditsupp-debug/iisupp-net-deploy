The product, in one sentence
ARIA Sentinel lives on your PC, watches for tech problems, and fixes them before they slow you down.
A single gold globe hugs the edge of the screen. It stays calm until something breaks — then it diagnoses, fixes, and gets out of the way.
How it works
Three modes, you stay in control
ManualDEFAULT · FREE
ARIA detects the issue and walks you through the fix in chat. Nothing is applied automatically.
ConfirmedPAID
ARIA proposes a fix card and applies it the moment you confirm.
AutonomousPAID · OPT-IN
ARIA silently fixes low-risk issues and tells you after. High-risk fixes still confirm.
When ARIA can't fix it
It raises a routed ServiceNow incident — automatically
ARIA classifies the unresolved signal and files an incident to the correct assignment group, pre-filled with diagnostics. Routing is policy-driven and not user-editable.

Architecture
Four layers, one engine
L4 · BRAND
Gold globe · Cinzel + Inter · ARIA TTS voice · dark only
L3 · BACKEND
iisupp.net — pull-only: signed recipes + BSOD stop-code index. No user data uploads.
L2 · BROWSER
Manifest V3 extensions — Chrome · Edge · Safari · localhost WS bridge to the agent
L1 · AGENT
Electron · watchers · PowerShell fix runner · encrypted KB · BSOD/WinRE · ServiceNow bridge
Trust
Privacy by engineering, not by promise
No user-data upload path exists in the binary — verifiable with any network monitor. Recipes come down from iisupp.net; incidents go to your own ServiceNow. KB is encrypted on-device. The verification harness is source-available.
