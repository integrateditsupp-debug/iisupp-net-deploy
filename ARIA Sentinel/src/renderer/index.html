Mode change

        
Switch to Autonomous?

        
Autonomous means ARIA fixes low-risk (green) issues without asking and tells you after. Higher-risk fixes still confirm with you every time, even in Autonomous.

        
    
          
  • Only green recipes auto-apply (DNS flush, service restarts, cache flush).
    • 
          
  • Same recipe runs at most 3× per 24h, with a 30-min cooldown.
    • 
          
  • A restore point is taken before any change.
    • 
          
  • Pause anytime from Settings → Mode.
