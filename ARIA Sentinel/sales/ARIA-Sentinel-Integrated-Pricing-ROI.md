**Access/password savings ≈ seats × $73.5 / year:**

| Seats | Access savings / yr (this flow alone) |

| 1,000 | **~$74,000** |

Add the rest of L1 automation (slow PC, Outlook, printer, VPN, OneDrive…) and total savings typically run **2–3× the access-only figure**. On access alone, Integrated pays for itself at roughly **1,500–3,000 seats** — sooner once full L1 deflection, avoided downtime, and reduced breach risk are counted.
