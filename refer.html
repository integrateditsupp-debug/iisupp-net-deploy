How it works

  1. You submit the referral below — name, email, company, why you think they would benefit. We do NOT contact them without your permission.
  2. We reply to you within 48h — confirm whether ARIA is a fit and ask if you want to make the warm intro yourself or have us reach out cold (with your reference).
  3. If they sign up within 90 days of the referral, we attribute them to you in our CRM and start paying out 10% of their monthly subscription starting from their first successful billing.
  4. Payouts continue for 12 months from their signup date, paid monthly via Interac (Canada) or wire (US/intl). We send an itemized statement each month.
  5. If they cancel within 12 months, payouts stop with their last paid month. No clawbacks.
